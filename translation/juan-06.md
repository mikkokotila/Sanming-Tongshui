# Sanming Tonghui · Juan Six
## A Comprehensive Gathering of the Three Fates

**Compiled by Wan Minying of the Ming dynasty.** From the imperially commissioned *Siku Quanshu*.[^01]

*Reading conventions.* Wood, Fire, Earth, Metal, and Water are the Five Phases. *Qi*, *yin*, *yang*, *nayin*, and **Lu** retain their technical meanings; stem–branch names use unaccented pinyin. Capitalized names such as Direct Officer, Seven Killings, Seal, Food God, Birth, and Extinction denote technical categories. **〈 〉** marks the source’s small-character commentary. Square brackets identify a supplied reading or a marked repair. The text’s predictions and judgments are translated as historical assertions, not as established facts about living people. Its alternative explanations remain distinct.[^02]

## 1. The Slanting Fork of the Well-Railing
### 井欄斜义

<!-- source: L14-L22 -->
〈The *Chapter on Preferences and Aversions* says:〉 “A Geng day fully meeting Moistening-Downward avoids Bing, Ding, Si, and Wu. If the hour meets Zi or Shen, its blessing is halved.” The configuration takes Gengshen, Gengzi, and Gengchen as its three principal days. The earthly branches form the triple Water combination, and three Geng emerge among the heavenly stems: this is “fully meeting Moistening-Downward.” Geng employs Ding as Officer; Shen–Zi–Chen clashes with the Yin–Wu–Xu Fire configuration, bringing Geng its Officer Star and distinction. Bing or Ding makes Officer or Killings openly present; Si or Wu fills the well’s mouth. Bingzi hour gives Indirect Officer upon the hour; Jiashen gives the Day’s Lu Returning to the Hour. These make the present configuration difficult to complete, so its blessing is incomplete and halved.[^03]

The well-railing’s fork is the well’s mouth; Moistening-Downward is Water. A well containing Water sustains people. Wu or Wei filling it makes Earth mingle with Water, taking away that sustaining power. Yin, Wu, or Xu in the month clashes and breaks the configuration: Water and Fire torment one another, and calamity follows instead. Ren or Gui in the stems draws Shen–Zi–Chen into Hurting Officer and removes the strength of Yin–Wu–Xu Fire. Wu or Ji injures the Water configuration so that it cannot clash forth the noble Fire of Yin–Wu–Xu. These conditions reduce the judgment; years and luck courses follow the same rule. For the configuration to be complete, not a trace of Fire qi should appear in the pillars, and birth should fall in autumn or winter. Wuchen or Wuzi does no harm. Gengzi again meeting Zi hour is judged simply as Flying Heavenly Lu and Horse; in Chen month, judge Seal-Ribbon; in Zi month, Hurting Officer. One must understand the transformations and assess waxing and waning. A genuine instance gives pure, exceptional distinction and manifest rank, though not very great wealth. Luck welcomes eastern Wealth and northern Hurting Officer, avoids southern Fire and Earth, and is ordinary in the west.[^04]

The rhapsody says: “Well-Railing Moistening-Downward finds excellence in three Geng; Wealth and Seal bring joy. Avoid Li’s palace and the Wu position; welcome Yin to invite the spirit. Filling the position gives splendor, wealth, and distinction; carrying Blade gives command of a thousand soldiers.” Examine this account: Geng takes Earth as Seal, yet Earth can fill the well’s mouth. Yin can clash with Shen, injuring the useful influence. How, then, can these make distinction? Consider it carefully.[^05]

A poem says:
> A Geng day fully meeting Shen, Zi, and Chen—  
> The Well-Railing’s fork rises above the crowd.  
> With Bing, Ding, Yin, and Wu wholly absent,  
> Here is wealth and distinction in a well-ordered court.

> The Well-Railing: a Geng day with Shen, Zi, and Chen.  
> Many Geng and the complete assembly establish the form.  
> Greatly fear Yin, Wu, and Xu breaking it;  
> Bing and Ding encountered are equally unkind.

> Well-Railing luck welcomes eastern ground;  
> Reaching Wealth’s country brings true wealth and distinction.  
> Bing, Ding, Si, and Wu in year or course  
> Threaten lost emolument and broken wealth.

> Shen, Zi, and Chen complete, the day meeting Geng:  
> The Well-Railing configuration restrains Officer Star.  
> Only without Fire in the assembly is it noble;  
> Breaking and disturbing the monthly framework brings calamity.

> Three Geng at birth renew auspicious qi;  
> Moistening-Downward complete makes the true Well-Railing.  
> Metal’s essence fears Yin, Wu, and Xu;  
> Water’s refinement welcomes Shen, Zi, and Chen.  
> Too much Ren and Gui injures noble qi;  
> Let no exposed Officer come with Bing and Ding.  
> In general, an eastern course is beautiful:  
> A lifetime of splendor, untouched by poverty.

## 2. Ren Riding the Dragon’s Back
### 壬騎龍背

<!-- source: L24-L26 -->
〈The *Chapter on Preferences and Aversions* says:〉 “Yang Water repeatedly meeting Chen is the country of Ren Riding the Dragon’s Back.” This configuration takes a Ren day seated upon Chen. Ren uses Ding as Wealth and Ji as Officer; the branch Chen secretly clashes forth Ding and Wu within Xu, bringing Ren the distinction of Wealth and Officer. Several Chen must occur to set the clash in motion. A further Yin holding Wealth and Officer by combination is excellent. Wealth and Officer should not appear openly. A course strengthening the body, or bringing Hurting Officer or Food God, is welcome; southern Wealth-and-Officer territory is avoided. If Ding, Si, Wu [午], or Xu appears in the pillars, judge Wealth and Officer directly instead. A Ren day seated upon Yin with many Chen also admits this configuration: Ren takes Jia as Food; Jia combines with Ji, Ren’s Officer, and generates Ding, Ren’s Wealth. Chen clashes with Xu and Yin combines with it, producing distinction. A Renchen day whose year, month, and hour all form Yin–Wu Fire has flourishing Wealth established upon its ground; much Wealth lacks purity, making wealth alone.[^06]

Another account says: a Renchen day uses many Chen to clash secretly forth Fire, Earth, and Metal in Xu—the three wonders Wealth, Officer, and Seal. Three Chen and one Yin make the clashing and combining noble qi powerful. If a Renchen day has Yin throughout year, month, and hour, the force is light; Jia within Yin is instead employed as Food generating Wealth, so wealth is indicated. Chou and Wei in the pillars favor distinction. Ji Officer, Wu Killings, Yi Hurting Officer, and Ding combining are greatly feared: they prevent the configuration, and even many Chen receive a reduced judgment. Northern Hai and Zi courses are avoided.[^07]

Another account says: Renchen is a Kuigang day. It welcomes bodily strength and fears Wealth and Officer; ease or obstruction must be assessed through the course. If Shen and Zi both appear, judge Moistening-Downward. Wu, Ji, Chen, and Xu in luck, with clashes and the annual and decadal influences arriving together, turn good into calamity: this is Riding the Dragon Running into Clashes, not the completed configuration. One chart is **Jichou / Wuchen / Renchen / Gengzi**: a provincial graduate in a Jiazi year, dead in the Yimao month of a Wuchen year. Ji Officer and Killings are excessively strong and control Ren, making calamity.

*Walking Alone* says: “Ren Riding the Dragon’s Back finds Xu unkind. Many Yin make wealth; many Chen, splendor.” The *Bright Mirror Rhapsody* says: “Ren Riding the Dragon’s Back welcomes Yin and Chen in mutual accord, and avoids Wu, Ji, Si, and Wu [午] as obstruction. Many Yin mean money filling the house and grain left to rot; purity carries the surname through the court.” The *Rhapsody on Examining the Heart* says: “Ren Riding the Dragon’s Back broken by Ding has desires like Shen Cheng.” The *Rhapsody on Portents* says: “Yang Water meeting Chen and seeing Wu or Ji cannot escape calamity.” The *Thousand-Li Horse* says: “Ren day, Ren hour, repeated Yin and Chen: high integrity receives imperial favor and ascends the royal pavilion.” The *Precious Mirror Rhapsody* says: “Shi Chong’s magnificent wealth: many Yin within the pillars.” The *Secret Instructions* say: “Among the Five Phases, Ren Riding the Dragon’s Back particularly welcomes Yin and Chen.”[^08]

A poem says:
> Birth upon Renchen is called Riding the Dragon;  
> Officer Star flies forth through the opposite clash.  
> Many Chen in the Four Pillars display rank and office;  
> Many Yin instead make a wealthy householder.

> Yang Water repeatedly meets Chen’s country;  
> Ren Riding the Dragon’s Back has exceptional distinction.  
> Yin and Chen both appearing in the pillars  
> Bring wealth and rank together in the halls of state.

> Ren Riding the Dragon’s Back is exceptionally fortunate;  
> Many Chen, with Yin, unfold its power.  
> Greatly avoid Officer Star breaking the configuration:  
> Calamity and punishment then injure the source of life.

> Renyin does not equal the Renchen day;  
> The Four Pillars need many Ren and Chen.  
> Many Chen give weighty office;  
> Many Yin can rival, even surpass, Shi Chong.

> Ren Riding the Dragon’s Back is exceptionally fortunate;  
> Layer upon layer of yang Water circles the land of Zheng.  
> Chen clashes forth refined qi from Xu;  
> Xu reaches Wu and enters Officer’s country.  
> Repeated dragons confer official rank;  
> Repeated tigers fill the granaries.  
> Above and below, the triple combination completes Water:  
> Wealth and distinction together are far beyond the ordinary.[^09]

*To the tune “Partridge Sky”:*
> Yang Water with repeated Chen is most auspicious;  
> Without Wu or Ji in the pillars, one sits in court.  
> Chen clashes forth noble Wealth and Officer within Xu;  
> Yin combining in the pillars gives them strength.  
> Holding the jade tablet, receiving the golden seal,  
> One orders the Four Seas and secures the frontier;  
> Honors the worthy, embraces the many, cultivates Heaven’s virtue—  
> Resounding authority and fame spread through the eight directions.

Censor-in-chief **Sun Piyang**, **Renchen / Jiachen / Renyin / Gengzi**, exemplifies this configuration.

## 3. Zi Reaching Afar for Si’s Lu
### 子遥巳禄

<!-- source: L28-L30 -->
〈The *Chapter on Preferences and Aversions* says:〉 “A Jiazi day again meeting Zi hour fears Geng, Xin, Shen, You, Chou, and Wu.” This configuration requires a Jiazi day and Jiazi hour. Jia takes Xin as Officer. Gui Water within the two Zi can reach afar to combine with Wu Earth within Si. Wu comes to combine with Gui, but fears control from Jia Wood above Zi and dares not do so. Wu and Bing share Si’s palace as father and son: when Wu moves, Bing also moves. Bing then combines with Xin within You, bringing Xin to control Jia Wood. Once Jia obtains Officer Star, Wu can combine with Gui. This is the Si–You–Chou triple assembly calling forth Officer Star. Wu [午] in year or month is greatly feared because it clashes with Zi; Chou binds Zi. Either prevents the reaching afar.[^10]

Another explanation says: Jia as ruler controls and calls forth Wu Earth within Si; Gui within Zi also controls and calls forth Bing Fire there. Wu, called forth through control, combines with Gui. Bing, called forth and without a partner, seeks Xin Metal within You to combine with it. The Jia day thus obtains the beauty of Wealth and Officer.

Another account says: Gui in the two Zi reaches afar to combine with Wu in Si, Jia’s Wealth. Bing and Wu share Lu at Si; Bing is Jia’s Rank Star, and Wu is Bing’s Rank Star. Wu moves and Bing moves too. Bing sees Zi; Wu desires combination with Gui as wife, [Gui serving Bing as] Seal-Ribbon. Bing then combines forth Xin as Jia’s Officer. It is like a man whose son continues his line and transmits his household, completing the dignity of fatherhood. Birth in Ren, Gui, Hai, or Zi months is welcome for flourishing Seal; Mao and Yin months, for a flourishing body. A course through flourishing Officer should bring examination success, emolument, and authority; a less pure form brings wealth. Geng, Xin, and Bing openly displayed, or Shen, You, and Si breaking the configuration, are avoided. If restraint or transformation exists, they do no harm. Chou binding and Wu clashing reduce the judgment. Years and courses follow the same rule.[^11]

Birth in You or Chou month is treated simply as Direct Officer; even a weakly exposed Geng can indicate wealth and distinction. Everything depends on the monthly command: Killings may generate and assist Seal. A genuine instance of the present configuration must observe its prohibitions, especially a southern course. Minister **Fan Jizu**, **Gengzi / Jimao / Jiazi / Jiazi**, is a true example.

The classic says: “Jiazi meeting Combining Lu must possess lifelong abundance.” A poem says:
> A Jiazi day meeting Jiazi hour  
> Reaches afar to combine Officer and Lu—distinction beyond doubt.  
> Chou binds, [Wu] clashes, Officer and Killings appear:  
> Blessing loses its promise and emolument comes late.[^12]

> A Jiazi day again meeting Jiazi hour:  
> Do not say flourishing Officer is unsuitable.  
> The birth month strengthens the Day Master’s root;  
> Luck reaching Metal’s country becomes exceptional.

> Jiazi day and Jiazi hour—  
> Who knows whence Officer suddenly arrives?  
> Gui moves Wu, and Wu moves Bing;  
> Bing combines with Xin: blessing’s foundation is born.  
> Geng, Xin, Shen, and You are strictly avoided;  
> Chou binding and Wu clashing are unwelcome too.  
> If these occur in pillars or luck,  
> The configuration fails and blessing is impaired.

> Zi reaching afar for Si requires deep reflection;  
> Let Jiazi seek Jiazi again.  
> Gui invites Wu Earth from Si;  
> Bing comes to You and combines with Xin Metal.  
> Hidden Shen and You welcome triple and Six Combination;  
> Open Geng and Xin—and the two Gui—are feared.  
> Without Chou or Wu, judge a lofty configuration:  
> Examination success brings the banquet in the Jade Grove.[^13]

## 4. Chou Reaching Afar for Si’s Lu
### 丑遥巳禄

<!-- source: L32-L34 -->
〈The *Chapter on Preferences and Aversions* says:〉 “Xin and Gui days repeatedly meeting Chou do not welcome Officer Star. Zi or Si in year or hour gives an empty name and empty gain.” Only Xinchou and Guichou belong to this configuration. Xin takes Bing as Officer, Gui takes Wu. Bing and Wu have Lu at Si, and Chou alone can break Si. Many Chou in the pillars bring forth Bing and Wu’s Lu; Xin and Gui combine with it from afar and obtain Officer Star. Zi binding, Wei clashing, and Si filling the absent position are avoided: they leave no more than empty reputation and profit. Years and luck courses are judged the same way.[^14]

Xinchou should be born in autumn, Guichou in winter. Abundant Metal and Water in the pillars complete the configuration. One Shen or You holding Si through combination is excellent, preventing its noble qi from escaping. For Xin, purity means no Bing, Ding, Si, or Wu [午]; for Gui, no Wu, Ji, Si, or Wu [午]. With neither clash nor binding, the person is honest and substantial, possessing wealth and distinction together; slight injury still allows abundance. Birth in Chen, Xu, Chou, or Wei month should instead be interpreted through Mixed Qi. Mao, Chen, Shen, You, or Hai hour likewise excludes this configuration. A Xin day in Bingyin, Bingwu, or Bingxu month is judged as Officer Star; in Jiayin month, Wood assists Fire, allowing Wealth and Officer to be used. A Gui day with much Earth is judged through Officer and Killings; Guihai hour, through Arching Lu; a month of flourishing Metal, through Seal-Ribbon; a month of flourishing Fire, through Wealth. Jiayin month’s Hurting Officer does no harm. A course in which Officer Star obtains its ground and the body flourishes often brings distinction. This configuration closely resembles Flying Heavenly Lu and Horse on Xinhai and Guihai days.

The classic says: “Xin and Gui days Combining Lu possess lifelong wealth to spare.” A poem says:
> Xin and Gui days meeting many Chou  
> Are called Reaching Afar for Si and Combining Officer Star.  
> Do not say they dislike flourishing Officer:  
> Who would believe Officer’s arrival instead brings success?

> Xinchou and Guichou are the two day pillars;  
> Chou can break Si, where Officer lies concealed.  
> Only many Chou make the arrangement excellent;  
> No Zi should stand among them.

> Xin and Gui without Officer: many Chou reach afar,  
> Calling Bing and Wu’s Lu from Si to court.  
> The branches welcome You and Shen in combination;  
> A fitting configuration must give abundant noble Lu.  
> Xin avoids Bing, Ding, Si, and Wu;  
> Gui dislikes Wu, Ji, Horse, and Snake—the baleful influences.  
> Zi comes to bind Chou and makes the heart truly idle;  
> If the configuration is slight, blessing likewise fades.[^15]

> How does Chou Reaching Afar for Si work?  
> Xin and Gui particularly need many Chou.  
> Shen and You met by Gui combine with Officer;  
> Rat and Snake wear blessing away.  
> Xin fears meeting Bing and Ding;  
> A Gui day finds Wu and Ji difficult companions.  
> If the foregoing prohibitions do not enter the Eight Characters,  
> One may stand alone at the highest examination rank.

## 5. Obtaining Lu through Punishment and Combination
### 刑合得禄

<!-- source: L36-L38 -->
〈The *Chapter on Preferences and Aversions* says:〉 “For the six Gui days, an Yin hour fears Wu and Ji in year or month.” Gui employs Wu Earth as Officer Star; Wu’s Lu is at Si. Jiayin hour punishes forth Wu Earth from Si, bringing Gui its Officer Star. Wealth Star or Seal’s assistance is welcome; courses of Wealth and Seal, punishment and clash, meeting and combination are all favorable. Wu or Ji among the year’s or month’s stems and branches openly displays Officer or Killings and reduces the judgment. If a year or course fills the absent position, or the allotted blessing has run its course, death follows. When Wu or Ji is already in the pillars, Void in the hour is welcome. If the monthly command occupies Direct or Indirect Officer’s position, however, the hour’s preferences and aversions no longer govern. Guihai, Guimao, and Guiwei seated upon a Wood configuration, with Jiayin hour and Wu, Ji, Si, or Wu [午] in the pillars, genuinely form Hurting Officer Meeting Officer: they do not qualify. Encountering these in years or courses brings calamity.[^16]

This closely resembles Flying Heavenly Lu and Horse. Since Officer is clashed forth, there should also be combination. One You or Chou in the pillars can combine with Si. Shen alone is not used, because it clashes with and controls Yin. Hai, Wu [午], or Xu [戌] creates binding; Si fills the position; Shen or Geng clashes and controls. These all break the arrangement and prevent distinction.[^17]

Another account says that the six Gui days may also use Gengshen hour to punish and combine forth Wu’s Lu in Si as Officer. The six Ji days may use Renshen hour to punish forth Jia’s Lu in Yin as Officer. A Xin day with Gengyin hour cannot complete the punishment: although Bing is Xin’s Officer and establishes Lu at Si, Geng controls Jia, injuring Yin so it cannot punish Si. Xin therefore obtains no Officer Star, and this is not accepted. The *Raising the Good* says: “Punishment-and-Combination born in the first month is simply Hurting Officer.” It also says: “If You or Chou appears in the pillars, the person is a hero of reputation and profit.”

A poem says:
> A Gui day born at Jiayin hour:  
> The borrowed name “Punishment-and-Combination” is not the whole truth.  
> If the monthly command adds Yin or Hai,  
> Seek its principle within Hurting-Officer configurations.

> Seek a Gui day with Jiayin hour:  
> Punish forth Officer Star, and distinction can be known.  
> Geng Metal injuring Jia Wood is unwelcome;  
> Yin–Shen clashing and breaking brings sorrow and danger.

> A Gui day without Officer, at Jiayin hour,  
> Needs Yin to punish forth Bing and Wu from Si.  
> Jia can control Wu, and Bing emerges of itself;  
> Gui obtains Wealth and Officer, completing noble Lu.  
> Let Wu and Ji be absent from the Four Pillars:  
> Then the form is complete, with a great and resounding name.  
> Geng comes, Killings appears, and Jia is injured:  
> Though property may remain, the person wears commoner’s white.

> Seek Punishment-and-Combination among the six Gui;  
> The birth hour welcomes Jiayin’s arrival.  
> Yin presses upon Si, and Wu should emerge;  
> Gui meets the Snake and obtains Officer’s charge.  
> Wu and Ji encountered readily bring calamity;  
> Geng and Shen arriving bring trouble hard to contain.  
> Its courses resemble the Flying Heavenly configuration;  
> With the forbidden injuries absent, blessing grows deeper.

Grand Secretary **Gao Gong**, **Renshen / Guichou / Guichou / Jiayin**, had Shen in the pillars and suffered illness. In Gengshen luck, meeting a Renshen year, he lost office and nearly met great calamity. The foregoing forms of reaching afar, hidden combination, punishment, and flying clashes belong to the *Source* tradition’s eighteen configurations. They apply only when Wealth and Officer are originally absent. Within and without the Four Pillars’ Three Origins, combination may be absent yet nevertheless obtained from another position: empty invitation, hidden arching, punishment, clash, breaking, and control combine with an alternative Lu. The foregoing configurations are examples.[^18] Punishment without combination cannot hold noble qi, and the configuration does not form.

## 6. Clashing and Combining Lu and Horse
### 衝合禄馬

<!-- source: L40-L42 -->
For a Jia day, the pillars lack You, Xin, and Chou but contain many Mao to clash with You; Si and You combine with Chou, supplying Jia’s Direct Officer. Ren and Gui generating assistance are welcome; You and Xin filling the position are avoided. Even one Mao, with further punishment and combination calling the influence forth, makes a good fate. For a Yi day, the pillars lack Shen and Geng but contain Yin to clash with Shen; Zi and Chen combine and arch, or Si punishes it forth, supplying Yi’s Direct Officer. Ren and Gui’s assistance is welcome; Shen and Geng filling the position are avoided. For a Wu day, the pillars lack Mao and Yi but contain You to clash with Mao; Hai and Wei combine with Mao, giving Wu its Officer Star. Ren and Gui Wealth and Bing and Ding Seal are welcome; Mao and Yi are avoided. For a Ji day, the pillars lack Yin and Jia but contain Shen to clash with Yin; Wu [午] and Xu combine with Yin, secretly supplying Officer Star. Wealth and Seal are welcome; Yin and Jia are avoided. Bing and Ding days follow Reversed-Clash Lu and Horse; Geng, Xin, Ren, and Gui follow Flying Heavenly Lu and Horse. Infer the cases accordingly.[^19]

For the four stems first discussed, clash and combination avoid Officer openly appearing or suffering injury. Officer obtaining the monthly command’s flourishing qi is excellent. The clashed-forth influence meeting combination, without entanglement, must bring pure distinction as general, chancellor, duke, or marquis. A missing condition reduces the judgment; a broken configuration brings livelihood through proximity to the powerful, or poverty in the gravest case. Years and luck courses have the same preferences and aversions.

The *Wubao Classic* says: “Consummate integrity is when the heavenly stem belongs to Wood, the branch-origin belongs to Wood, and the human origin belongs to Wood. Such a person cannot be corrupted by wealth and rank, moved from principle by poverty and low station, or bent by power and force: a great man indeed.” Yin and yang must be matched. It is like the six gates: half a jin of yang stems and half a jin of yin stems make a pair. Partiality prevents creative transformation from reaching completion. A pure, steadfast, quiet woman must be matched with an upright, worthy husband. Yimao’s Wood, flourishing in Lu, will not unite with the Metal of Gengwu, Gengyin, Gengzi, or Gengchen. Such declining and defeated Metal cannot combine with flourishing Yimao Wood. What is to be done? Wait until luck reaches Shen or You, Metal’s flourishing country. Yi Wood then follows the season, like a steadfast woman awaiting flourishing Gengshen as her mate. Gengwu, Gengyin, Gengzi, and Gengchen cannot subdue this faithful woman to become their wife.[^20]

Yi, for example, employs Geng and Shen as husband. If absent from the pillars, Zi and Chen can combine forth Geng within Shen as Yi’s Officer Star. Yin’s clash is then unnecessary, although Yin makes the arrangement finer. Without Zi and Chen, the clashing-forth method is used. **Jiayin, Yimao, Bingwu, Dingwei, Wuwu, Jiwei, Gengshen, Xinyou, Renzi, and Guihai** are ten days with vigorous constitutions; they clash Lu’s origin forth most powerfully. **Renchen, Renxu, Wuchen, and Jichou** do so more slowly. The remaining day stems are not used. 〈Ming Emperor Shenzong’s chart is **Guihai / Xinyou / Guihai / Xinyou**. Guihai as ruler and root is vigorous, clashing forth Bing and Wu—Lu and Horse—from Si for Gui’s use. The two You hold them by combination: this is its excellence.〉

## 7. Breaking Forth Officer
### 破官

<!-- source: L44-L46 -->
The Four Pillars originally have no Wealth, Officer, or Seal-Ribbon, but contain a branch that breaks forth Officer. A Guimao day breaks forth Ji Earth in Wu [午] as Officer; Guiyou breaks forth [Wu] Earth in Chen; Jiawu breaks forth Xin Metal in You. One branch of a triple combination must hold noble qi in the pillars: this is excellent. The *Rhapsody on the Original Principle* says: “Mao breaks Wu, Wu breaks You: Wealth and Officer are both beautiful.” It also says: “When the year and day lack a branch breaking forth Officer, but month and hour contain branches that break forth and combine Officer, distinction is indicated.” A Jiayin day has no such breaking, but Bingwu month and Jisi hour use Wu [午] to break forth Xin Officer in You, with Si combining it. Bing and [Wu] within Si are also used to combine Xin and make distinction.[^21]

A poem says:
> Mao breaks Wu and Wei, and great Officer appears;  
> Wu and Wei breaking You are judged the same.  
> Chou breaking Si and Wu does not follow this example;  
> Zi breaking Mao and Chen is not difficult to employ.

## 8. Flying Wealth
### 飛財

<!-- source: L48-L50 -->
The day stem matches the month stem, and the day branch matches the hour branch, clashing forth Wealth from the opposite palace. This configuration should develop wealth and emolument; drainage of qi is ominous. A Renshen day with Shen in the hour has two Shen clashing forth Jia and Bing within Yin as Wealth and the useful influence. Zi encountered in a year or course transforms them into a Water configuration. If the day stem is Geng, the configuration drains qi as Hurting Officer and cannot generate Wealth; further Seven Killings in the annual ruler or natal pillars controlling the body must bring death. The branches of Flying Wealth must not transform into another configuration.[^22]

Take **Wuyin / Jiwei / Wuyin / Jiayin**: three Yin and one Jia give Seven Killings, yet the sixth month has no Wealth qi at all and Companions are very strong. The three Yin clash forth Water at Birth within Shen as Wealth. Luck traveling northwest gives immense property and wealth.

## 9. Breaking Forth Wealth
### 破財

<!-- source: L52-L54 -->
A Yimao day, for example, has no Wealth or Officer among the Eight Characters and uses Mao to break forth Ji Earth within Wu [午] and Wei as Wealth. One Yin or Xu secretly combining Wealth’s qi is excellent. Rob Wealth and filling the position are avoided. An actual Wealth position, or an actual position filling the clash, breaks the configuration and makes it ineffective. Gengshen and Xinyou days break forth Wood from Yin, Mao, and Chen as Wealth; Bingwu and Dingwei break forth Metal from You and Xu; Renzi and Guichou break forth Fire within Si and Wu. These days possess their own Day Blade or Day Lu: strong in themselves, they can clash and break forth Wealth for use. How could a yielding, weak day ruler seize unexpected wealth by breaking it forth? Those fitting the configuration can often rise suddenly through wealth they had not anticipated.[^23]

A poem says:
> With no Wealth in the fate, examine Breaking Wealth;  
> Wealth and Lu broken forth pile up like hills.  
> Luck traveling Officer and Seal gives blessing upon blessing;  
> Punishment, clash, and filling the position threaten calamity.

> Mao breaks Wu and Wei to obtain Wealth;  
> Wu and Wei breaking You follow the same rule.  
> Chou breaks Si and Wu, and wealth arrives abundantly;  
> You breaks Chen and Mao, and blessing comes readily.

## 10. Mao and Wei Reaching Afar for Si
### 卯未遥巳

<!-- source: L56-L58 -->
This configuration takes **Xinmao, Guimao, Xinwei, and Guiwei**. Xin and Gui combine with Bing and Wu within Si as Officer. Three or four Mao in succession must clash with You, or three or four Wei with Chou. You and Chou then secretly combine with Bing and Wu in Si, the Officer Stars of Xin and Gui days. Si filling the position is greatly avoided; years and courses follow the same rule. With only one or two occurrences, this configuration is not used: judgment follows Wealth, Officer, Noble Person, or Food alone.

## 11. Tiger and [Ox] Hastening to Si
### 虎午奔巳

<!-- source: L60-L62 -->
A Xin or Gui day meets Chou and Yin. Yin supplies punishment, Chou combination; together they punish and combine forth Bing and [Wu] within Si as Xin’s and Gui’s Officer Stars. ## 12. The Goat Striking Pig and Snake
### 羊擊猪蛇

This configuration takes **Xinwei and Guiwei** days. Two or three Wei combine forth Hai, which clashes forth Bing and [Wu] in Si as the Officers of Xin and Gui. One You or Chou holding noble qi by combination is excellent. Filling the position, clash, and punishment are feared. **Jiaxu / Xinwei / Guiwei / Guichou** and **Gengshen / Guiwei / Xinwei / Yiwei** both fit the configuration and give distinction.

The foregoing configurations require Wealth and Officer to be absent from the pillars; otherwise they are not used. Luoluzi says: “Combination where no combination appears is hard for later students to know; obtaining one and dividing into three was not recorded by earlier worthies.” Yin and Xu imply Wu; Shen and Chen imply Zi. This is obtaining one and dividing into three. Examine whether it brings blessing or calamity to the natal origin. A complete triple combination can also fork forth something from the opposite palace, for good or ill: Yin–Wu–Xu forks forth Gui Water within Zi; Shen–Zi–Chen, Ding Fire within Wu. ## 13. Punishment and Clash Carrying Combination
### 刑衝帶合

<!-- source: L68-L70 -->
“Punishment” is the Three Punishments; “combination” is the Six Combinations. Punishment is like arsenic, combination like honey. People know honey’s sweetness but not the poison hidden within it. Examples are Jiazi meeting Jimao, Bingzi meeting Xinmao, and Gengshen meeting Yisi: the stems combine while the branches punish—combination above, punishment below. A fate offending this often indulges in wine and sexual pleasure. In its heavy form it destroys person and household; in its light form it produces illness, with no change even in old age. A thoroughly formed configuration may still make a great fate of power and distinction, but cannot avoid illness from drink and desire or disorder within the household. A lowly, thinly endowed fate remains lost in flowers and wine, wandering without accomplishment. Luck bringing baleful sha to control the body often ends life. It is especially avoided in women’s charts.[^27]

A poem says:
> Branches punishing while stems combine are most unfitting;  
> Wine and desire injure the body, calamity follows.  
> Even one who holds a minister’s cauldron  
> May imperil himself through love of beauty.

## 16. The Day’s Lu Returning to the Hour
### 日禄歸時

<!-- source: L80-L82 -->
〈The *Chapter on Preferences and Aversions* says:〉 “The Day’s Lu Returning to the Hour, without Officer Star, is called Finding the Road to the Azure Clouds.” There are seven day-and-hour cases: **Jia with Yin, Ding with Wu [午], Wu with Si, Ji with Wu [午], Geng with Shen, Ren with Hai, and Gui with Zi**. The Day Master’s Lu returns to the hour position. A flourishing seat for the day stem, a birth month of Seal-Ribbon, exposed Wealth, Hurting Officer or Food, and Heavenly and Monthly Virtue are welcome, indicating great wealth and distinction. Punishment, clash, breaking, harm, Void, Death, Extinction, Rob Wealth dividing Lu, Inverted Food forming combination, and Officer or Killings controlling are avoided. Though such a chart may still be usable, it is not pure. Years and luck courses follow the same rule.[^36]

A Yi day at Jimao hour has Indirect Wealth upon the hour; a Bing day at Guisi has exposed Officer Star; a Xin day at Dingyou has Indirect Officer upon the hour. These are not treated as Returning Lu; examine the Four Pillars. Officer Star in the month, or Wealth and Officer exposed among the heavenly stems, calls for ordinary Wealth-and-Officer analysis. Lu returning to the hour, with Lu also in the year, month, or hour branches, is **Gathered Blessing Returning Lu**, also called **Five Phases Returning Lu**. If the Day’s Lu returns to the hour and the Hour’s Lu to the day, it is **Exchanged Lu**. If the Year’s Lu returns to the hour and the Hour’s Lu to the year—as Jiashen meeting Gengyin, Yiyou meeting Xinmao, Renwu meeting Dinghai, or Guihai meeting Renzi—all indicate great distinction and enjoyment of blessing.

Repeated Lu positions—as Jia day at Yin hour also born in the first month—leave Wealth and Officer weak and are judged as Established Lu. If the month and day share a heavenly stem and only the hour supplies Lu, it is **Divided Lu**, rendered ineffective. If each returns separately to its own Lu, however, there is no harm. This configuration has seven methods.

**1. Finding the Road to the Azure Clouds.** **Wuzi / Jiayin / Yihai / Jimao** contains no trace of Officer Star. The body is strong, a configuration forms, and Seal gives assistance. Although Zi punishes Mao’s Lu, it cannot break the whole arrangement, so distinction follows. Again, **Renwu / [Gengxu] / Renzi / Xinhai** has a strong body and Seal’s help. The person passed the metropolitan examination in a Wuchen year and became a bureau director in the Ministry of Justice. It is even more fitting to call this Mixed Qi: the success came in Dingchou luck and the Wuchen examination year, with Chou punishing and Chen clashing.[^37]

**2. Officer Star Seated upon Lu.** **Bingshen / Bingshen / Bingshen / Guisi** takes Gui as Bing’s Direct Officer. Born in the seventh month, in Metal’s country, Officer has support. Luck travels northwest, Officer’s Birth and flourishing ground. Bing at Shen lacks qi, but the three Bing rely upon one another and clash with Yin, their Birth ground. Gui Officer at Si places the useful influence upon nobility and obtains the combined beauty of Wealth and Officer. Thus the person passes the examinations young and becomes chief minister in middle age.

**3. Returning Lu Meeting the Two Virtues.** **Xinhai / Xinmao / Jiayin / Bingyin** gives Jia exclusive Lu, while Bingyin is Food Meeting Lu. Jia is Monthly Virtue. Xinmao in the monthly command is Jia’s Direct Officer, but Xin lacks qi in the second month. Bing within the two Yin combines away the two Xin, leaving the two Yin as Jia’s Lu. Monthly Virtue meeting Returning Lu makes a heroic chief minister assisting the state.

**4. Returning Lu Meeting Seal-Ribbon.** **Dingwei / Renzi / Jiazi / Bingyin** has two Zi giving flourishing Seal. Ding and Ren transform into Wood and draw into Yin hour, where Wood flourishes. If luck meets Officer or Killings, Bing and Ding restrain or combine them, so they cannot damage the arrangement: hence distinction. Again, **Renyin / Bingwu / Jiachen / Bingyin** has the Day’s Lu Returning to the Hour without Officer or Killings. Bing serves as Food, welcome at Birth in Yin and flourishing in Wu. Ji Earth in Wu is Wealth. Seal-Ribbon in the year stem flourishes in its Storehouse at the day. Thus the person inherits ancestral protection, receives office, and gains wealth and distinction. Again, **Bingxu / Guisi / Wuwu / Dingsi** contains no trace of Officer Star. Bing and Ding support Wu as Seal; Lu is exchanged between day and hour. Wu, Xu, and Si are all Fire ground, so Seal generates the body excessively. Luck traveling west gives Food God and Hurting Officer generating Wealth, while Gui combines with Wu to form a Fire image. Hence early examination success and office of the third rank.

**5. Returning Lu Meeting Hurting Officer.** Officer is greatly avoided. **Renchen / Yisi / Jihai / Gengwu** has Jia Officer within Hai beneath Ji, but Si clashes it away. Yi is Killings, but Geng combines with it into True Metal, Hurting Officer. Ren is used as Wealth, seated in its Storehouse and returning to Lu beneath the day. Southern luck strengthens the body; western luck strengthens Metal. Hurting Officer removes Killings and generates usable Wealth qi: a distinction of inspired intelligence and substantial refinement.

**6. Returning Lu Meeting Killings.** **Jiashen / Bingyin / Wushen / Dingsi** places Wu upon Shen, its own Birth ground. Jia in the year stem emerges as Killings and returns to Lu in Yin, restrained by the two Shen. Wu returns to Lu in the hour without Officer mixing with it. Hence military authority and power securing the frontier.

**7. Returning Lu Meeting Wealth.** **Jihai / Bingyin / Dingchou / Bingwu** is seated upon the Wealth Storehouse. Bing seizes Wealth, but Ren in Hai restrains it; Yin and Hai combine. When luck encounters Officer or Killings, flourishing Companions can bear the charge. Ji Earth Food God returns to Lu in the hour and restrains Officer and Killings. Xu, You, and Shen courses all occupy flourishing Wealth territory: hence distinction. Again, **Jiazi / Bingzi / Wuzi / Dingsi** has three Zi as abundant, flourishing Wealth. Jia in the year stem emerges as Killings and receives generation. Wu Earth returning to Lu in the hour and obtaining ground is therefore welcome. Fire Seal in the month stem also returns to Lu in the hour: hence great distinction.[^38]

*Walking Alone* says: “The Day’s Lu Returning to the Hour finds the road to the azure clouds; Wealth and Officer in the monthly command lend auspicious assistance.” The *Rhapsody on the Original Principle* says: “Returning Lu obtains Wealth and wins blessing; without Wealth, Returning Lu still means poverty.” It also says: “The Day’s Lu Returning to the Hour does not welcome Officer Star anywhere in the Four Pillars, years, or courses. Punishment and harm halve the blessing.” The *Bright Mirror* says: “Officer or Killings makes the azure-cloud road difficult; punishment and harm attached to Traveling Horse bring honor to the person.” Huzhongzi says: “Worn upon the head and trodden beneath the feet: rarely one meets Jia year with Yin hour.”[^39]

A poem says:
> The Day Master returns to Lu in the birth hour,  
> Without clash, punishment, or falling into Void.  
> Officer and Killings absent, Wealth and Seal flourishing,  
> Hurting Officer, Food, and a vigorous body give a thousand measures of Lu.

> The six Jia have Lu at Yin;  
> Officer’s luminary arriving makes distinction hard to extend.  
> A body lacking vigor welcomes generating Seal;  
> Lu in excess loves Food God.

> Control and breaking leave blessing without a source.

## 17. Arching Lu and Arching Nobility
### 拱禄拱貴

<!-- source: L84-L86 -->
〈The *Chapter on Preferences and Aversions* says:〉 “Arching Lu and Arching Nobility become ominous when the position is filled.” *Arching* means facing toward or flanking. Lu is the Lu of Arrival at Office; nobility is Officer Star’s distinction, or sometimes Tianyi Noble Person. There are five day-and-hour pairs arching Lu: **Guihai–Guichou** and **Guichou–Guihai** arch Zi Lu; **Dingsi–Dingwei** and **Jiwei–Jisi** arch Wu [午] Lu; **Wuchen–Wuwu** arches Si Lu. Five day-and-hour pairs arch nobility: **Jiashen–Jiaxu** arches You; **Yiwei–Yiyou** arches Shen—these are Officer Nobility. **Jiayin–Jiazi** arches Chou; **Wushen–Wuwu**, Wei; **Xinchou–Xinmao**, Yin—Officer Nobility together with Tianyi Nobility.[^40]

In any arching configuration, day and hour must share the same stem, and the noble influence or Lu must communicate with the monthly command’s qi. Courses strengthening the body and the noble influence or Lu are very good; Seal-Ribbon, Hurting Officer, Food God, and Wealth courses are also auspicious. Punishment, clash, breaking, harm, Yang Blade, and Seven Killings are avoided: they injure day and hour so these cannot hold noble qi. Filling the position and Void are greatly avoided. Think of a vessel: empty, it can receive; already full, it has no use. Thus only empty arching is suitable. Intact, it can hold; broken, it is useless. Hence the fear of Void. Years and courses follow the same rule.[^41]

Another account says: in empty arching, examine what is arched forth from absence and exactly what it represents for one’s household—an auspicious spirit, an ominous sha, or another category. Zi and Yin arch Chou, giving Jia day Officer Star. But Wei clashing with Chou makes the flanking unstable and lets noble qi escape. Without Wei, pressure from Chen and Xu is urgent but still permits some hope. Hai and Mao make the arching qi firm; the absence of one leaves partial withering and disorder. This is a **Small-Scope Configuration**. Chou and Yin together with Si and Wu may flank Mao and Chen, closely enclosing and attending them. If Mao and Chen contain one’s noble qi and other stems awaken their favorable relationships, this is a **Middle-Scope Configuration**. Arching an entire eastern, western, southern, or northern domain is a **Great-Scope Configuration**: Si–Wu–Wei naturally contains southern Fire qi; Yin–Mao–Chen naturally contains eastern Wood qi. Unless the configuration is mighty and its constitution truly capacious, one must not apply such a broad inference indiscriminately.[^42]

The rhapsody says: “Weighty Lu and manifest rank surely arise in a country flanking Lu.” Guichou meeting Guihai, for example, arches Gui’s Lu at Zi. Born in autumn or winter, that Lu is substantial and possesses qi. Wuzi meeting Jiayin also arches noble Chou, but Wu is controlled by Jia: how could they arch it? Infer the others accordingly. *Walking Alone* says: “Arching Lu and Arching Nobility become ominous when filled; when the monthly framework has a useful influence, the judgment differs.” The *Three Fates* says: “Flanking Lu and flanking Nobility must reach the exalted Eight Seats.” The *Secret Instructions* say: “Strong stems flanking Lu and Nobility make an upright officer.” The *Bright Mirror* says: “Pure Arching Lu and Arching Nobility belong to kings and marquises; filled positions give empty reputation and gain. Without Wealth and Seal there is no joy; injury and harm, Officer and Killings, and Void are feared.”

A poem says:

> The golden coffer hides a pearl: a supremely pure form.  
> Highest, noblest is this gentleman’s fate,  
> Reaching duke or minister without care or fear.

> Secretly arching noble spirit and Lu’s position,  
> Meet neither filling nor Void.  
> Clash, punishment, Yang Blade, and Seven Killings  
> Break Officer Star beyond endurance.

> Arching Lu and Arching Nobility are rare configurations;  
> Still examine the monthly branch as framework.  
> If the framework is useful, it carries greater weight;  
> With no useful spirit in the month, employ this wonder.

> The arched position fears being filled;  
> It also fears Hurting Officer in the monthly branch.  
> Repeated Yang Blades break the configuration;  
> Without such breaking, distinction is certain.

## 18. Clashing Forth Lu

Geng has Lu at Shen. If Shen is absent but the day is Gengyin, with further Yin in year, month, or hour, they clash together to produce Shen as Geng’s Lu. Jia has Lu at Yin. If Yin is absent but the day is Jiashen, with further Shen in year, month, or hour, they clash together to produce Yin as Jia’s Lu. ## 20. The Six Jia Hastening toward Qian

The six Jia days meeting Hai form this configuration. Hai occupies Heaven’s Gate, the enclosure of the northern pole. ## 21. The Combined Beauty of Wealth and Officer
### 財官䨇美

<!-- source: L100-L102 -->
The *Continuing the Good* says: “The six Ren born upon Wu [午] are called Lu and Horse Sharing a Home; a Gui day seated in Si is Wealth and Officer Both Beautiful.” Lu is Officer, Wealth is Horse: the two statements have one meaning. Ren takes Ding Fire as Wealth-Horse and Ji Earth as Officer-Lu; both have Lu at Wu. Gui takes Bing Fire as Direct Wealth and Wu Earth as Direct Officer; both have Lu at Si. Lu and Horse, Wealth and Officer, are hard to obtain together in a fate—how much more precious when seated beneath the day itself. Autumn birth is welcome: Metal flourishes, Water is generated, and Wood dies and cannot control Earth, keeping injury distant. Flourishing Yin or Mao gives promise without fulfillment. Winter birth makes Dark Warrior Holding Authority, with rank as king or marquis. Wealth and Officer elsewhere in the pillars, together with one of these two days, are especially excellent. **Jichou / Dingmao / Renwu / Guimao** exposes Ding and Ji in year and month, returning them to Lu beneath the day, and fits this greatly noble form.[^46]

Luoluzi says: “Lu and Horse Sharing a Home reaches the Three Terraces or the Eight Seats.” Again: “Whenever a noble person receives emolument, Lu and Horse invariably share a home.” **Jiaxu, Yichou, Yisi, Bingshen, Dingchou, Wuchen, Jihai, Gengyin, Xinwei, Renxu, and Guiwei** also conceal Wealth and Officer beneath the day and thus share Lu and Horse’s home. The classic singles out Renwu and Guisi because they sit upon Direct Wealth and Direct Officer; the others mix direct and indirect and are not wholly pure.

By extension, Jiaxu and Yichou welcome Metal-and-Earth months for wealth and distinction. Metal must not be excessive lest it injure the body and steal qi. Even without official rank, wealth must develop. Bingshen and Dingchou are noble in Metal-and-Wood months; heavy Earth is their particular aversion. An Earth assembly controlling Officer gives wealth. Jihai should be born in a seasonal-ending month, or have generating support on which to rely. Gengyin welcomes Fire, especially birth after the winter solstice, when one yang begins and Fire grows strong, giving distinction. Firm Metal and strong Fire forge an edged instrument; autumn birth meeting Fire is especially fine.

Minister **Tang’s** chart, **Bingwu / Gengzi / Renwu / Bingwu**, has Ren born in Zi month with a flourishing body, clashing and moving Bing in the year stem. The two Bing share a lodging and clash away the Geng Owl influence. Geng, controlled and clashed, avoids Bing and moves onto the natal day’s Wu [午]; Bing in the hour moves onto Zi in the month. Ren is Geng’s child and moves onto the hour’s Wu. Thus they become **Bingwu / Bingzi / Gengwu / Renwu**, all Lu and Horse Sharing a Home. This is also Water and Fire Achieving Completion, and is called the Six Ren Exchanging Positions: hence great distinction. In general, a supremely noble fate may fit two or three configurations and find resources on every side. One must not call it mixed merely because it has several forms, as the *Source* does.[^47]

Again, **Jiazi / Bingyin / Renxu / Xinchou** seats Ren upon Xu, whose Ding and Wu are Wealth and Officer. Fire in Xu is only beginning to generate Earth, which meets Illness in Yin month. But Xinchou hour supplies Chou to harm Wu [午], while Zi clashes with Wu. Clash and harm bring Ding and Ji forth from Wu as Ren’s Wealth and Officer; Yin and Xu then combine and hold Wu’s Lu and Horse. Fire obtains a configuration through Yin and Xu; Ji Earth obtains its position within Chou. Both possess qi. Luck traveling south makes Lu and Horse flourish. This single Chou turns the chart into Three Wonders, Flying Heavenly Lu and Horse, and Combination Where No Combination Appears: hence great distinction.

Again, **Bingwu / Jiawu / Renxu / Bingwu** has Ren born in the fifth month, a day stem without qi, and excessive Fire. Judge it as Ding and Ren Following Fire. A western course, where Officer flourishes, displays the beauty of its Transformation Image. Ren itself is weak, yet three Wu break forth Xin Metal within You as Seal and clash forth Yang Blade in Zi to support the body, enabling it to bear Wealth and Officer. Throughout life there is not an idle day: one office arrives before the previous one is left. But Gengzi luck reaches Fire’s Death ground; Geng is Owl, while flourishing Water injures the source of support. Fire has no qi, Earth is washed about, Water strikes, and the position of the empowering Blade is filled. Hence adversity. The formula says: “Born where there is Death, released where there is flourishing.”[^48]

Ziping says: “Three Wonders and Lu and Horse Sharing a Home require a birth hour outside rest and Defeat.” An old song says:
> Lu and Horse sharing a home, without control or seizure—  
> Wealth and Officer in one place are most glorious.  
> The Three Terraces and Eight Seats give true, exceptional rank;  
> Strong control and seizure diminish profit and fame.

Follow the official calendar: only after the middle solar term, when the sun passes into the next palace, is the general encountered. This is precisely the meaning of placing the monthly general over the hour in the Six Ren method.[^52]

## 24. Metal Spirit
### 金神

<!-- source: L112-L114 -->
**Metal Spirit** is the influence of breaking and defeat, namely Target Sha. It occurs in just three hours: **Guiyou, Jisi, and Yichou**. The configuration primarily concerns the six Jia days; these three hours are then interpreted as Metal Spirit. Jiazi and Jiachen are best. Only when the monthly command communicates with [Metal] qi and a Fire configuration forms can this be employed. Seven Killings, Hurting Officer, or Blade in the pillars makes a truly noble person. Without the appropriate Metal qi and Fire configuration, judge another form—Wealth, Officer, Seal-Ribbon, transformation, or Following a Category. Even Water, ordinarily avoided, may then be acceptable. Without transformation, however, a course through Water country brings calamity beyond telling.[^55]

Another account says: fierceness takes force as its capacity. Unless authority is exclusive, others can insult it. Yet what is too hard must break. Without restraint, gentleness and fierceness, firmness and yielding cannot aid one another: how then can the way of balance be followed? If it is regulated, tamed, and brought to the mean, blessing and emolument arrive in succession. This configuration gives clear perception, quick judgment, firmness, decisiveness, and an unbending will. A Fire configuration in the Four Pillars, followed by a Fire-country course, is judged in this way. Birth in Hai, Mao, or Wei month followed by Fire luck also qualifies. Birth in a Water month, or a course through Water country, does not.

Birth in Chen month with northern luck can be judged as Seal-Ribbon: Officer, Killings, and Yang Blade are welcome; punishment and clash are feared. Guiyou hour places You at Jia’s Officer position. Do not judge it by saying Metal Spirit requires Fire restraint; treat it as Direct Officer. Courses in which Wealth and Officer obtain ground develop blessing. If Shen and Geng recur in year and month and Officer mixes with Killings, return to Metal-Spirit analysis: Fire in years and courses must bring blessing, Water calamity. Fire already in the pillars without a Fire course still has difficulty developing. Wealth and Wealth luck are welcome too. The six Ji days at these three hours are also called Metal Spirit: Metal-and-Water luck immediately brings calamity, Wealth luck is beautiful, and Fire country finer still.[^56]

Minister of War **Fang Fengshi**, **Renwu / Yisi / Jiachen / Jisi**, has Jia meeting Jisi with Metal Spirit in command. Pure Si–Wu Fire restrains it properly. ## 26. Day Virtue
### 日德

<!-- source: L120-L122 -->
Only five days belong to this configuration: **Jiayin, Bingchen, Wuchen, Gengchen, and Renxu**. It takes the five yang stems Jia, Bing, Wu, Geng, and Ren. Jia seated upon Yin obtains Lu; Bing upon Chen occupies Officer’s Storehouse; Geng upon Chen has Wealth and Seal together; Ren upon Xu possesses the Three Wonders. Yin heads the three yang, and Chen and Xu are Kuigang’s ground. Their stems and branches differ from other positions, hence the name Day Virtue. Yet among the one hundred and twenty-five auspicious influences I have examined, there is no Day Virtue. Perhaps the name has another derivation; I do not understand it.[^60]

Day Virtue must be numerous: two or three corresponding positions appearing together are required. With only one, return to Wealth, Officer, Seal, and Food in the monthly command. What obtains its position and season? What is injured and loses its season? Take what is usable and discard what is not. If Day Virtue truly forms, the person is kindly, charitable, imposing in physique, compassionate toward the poor and respectful toward elders, without harmful or exploitative intent. Misfortune finds rescue, difficulty resolution; violent calamity does not occur, and blessing must be abundant. The rhapsody says: “Day Virtue has a good heart, much steadiness and generosity, and kindness in its undertakings.”

Luck strengthening the body is exceptionally fine. But if flourishing qi has already declined, Kuigang encountered in the course must bring death. Or, before blessing has developed, even a fine configuration must encounter trouble when luck reaches Kuigang. Once free of this, it can develop again, though its ultimate strength is slight. A single Day Virtue welcomes Wealth and Officer; repeated Day Virtue should not meet them. Punishment, clash, breaking, harm, Void, and Kuigang meeting or descending are all greatly avoided.

Transport deputy **Zhang Zhu**, **Jiashen / Wuchen / Wuchen / Renxu**, rose from an educational office to wearing gold and purple and receiving a fifth-rank commission: this is the configuration. Renwu luck enters Wealth country, and Yang Blade within Wu holds power: all offend Day Virtue’s prohibitions. ## 27. Kuigang
### 魁罡

<!-- source: L124-L126 -->
This configuration has four days: **Gengchen, Renchen, Wuxu, and Gengxu**. Chen is Heavenly Gang; Xu is River Chief. They are the ground where yin and yang reach extinction, hence the name Kuigang. Jia alone is excluded: as head of the stems, it is Azure Dragon at Chen and Lu Hall at Xu, auspicious without being ominous. Repetition and repeated arrival of these day positions are required; their concentrated power makes distinction. The classic says: “Kuigang gathered in numbers develops extraordinary blessing.” It indicates intelligence, vigorous literary expression, decisiveness in affairs, authority, and a taste for killing. The rhapsody says: “Kuigang is stern in nature, principled in conduct, intelligent and quick.”[^63]

Body-strengthening luck develops blessing in a hundred forms. Wealth or Officer appearing immediately brings trouble, worse still with punishment or Killings. If the day position stands alone and punishment, clash, control, and restraint recur heavily, the person must be lowly, subject to endless punishment and bone-deep poverty. Luck reaching flourishing Wealth or Officer requires guarding against exceptional calamity. But if the monthly command supplies Wealth, Officer, or Seal-Ribbon and only the day has Kuigang, employ Wealth, Officer, Seal, or Food. Even slight breaking does no great harm when these obtain their positions. Weigh the monthly framework and take what is appropriate; do not be confined by this minor distinction.

Another account says: Gengxu and Gengchen have no Officer Star. Repeated Kuigang in favorable relationship makes wealth greater than reputation, but Wealth appearing prevents the configuration; its recurrence in flourishing Wealth luck brings unpredictable calamity. Gengchen born in the ninth month may attain distinction despite the Chen–Xu clash, when luck goes south and Fire is in the pillars. Gengxu born in the third month does not employ even Officer and Seal: Fire in Xu is Geng’s Officer Storehouse, Wu Earth its Seal, but Gui in Chen hurts Officer and drains Geng, so the form does not complete. Wuxu without Wealth is not noble and should not see Officer; repeated Kuigang in favorable relationship gives wealth and distinction together. Renchen fears Wealth and Officer and greatly welcomes Seal-Ribbon, Rob Wealth, and Killings. Years and courses follow the same rules.[^64]

Another account says: Chen is Water’s Storehouse and belongs to Heavenly Gang; Xu is Fire’s Storehouse and belongs to Earthly Chief. Chen and Xu encountering one another are **Heaven Clashing and Earth Striking**. The *General Discussion of Ziping* says: “A person meeting Heavenly Gang and Earthly Chief is bone-poor when weak, peerlessly distinguished when strong.”

A poem says:
> Renchen, Gengxu, and Gengchen,

I observe that this configuration always uses Chen and Xu, with only slight differences among the heavenly stems. Gengchen [appears under both headings], called Day Virtue as well as Kuigang, yet the configurations’ interpretations differ greatly. One need not insist upon these labels. Assistant commissioner **Zhang Shi**, **Gengwu / Dinghai / Wuxu / Bingchen**, and vice minister **Liu Dashou**, **Dinghai / Guichou / Gengxu / Wuyin**, both have Kuigang days but are judged only through Wealth, Officer, and Seal.[^65]

## 28. Blessed Virtue and Refined Qi
### 福德秀氣

<!-- source: L128-L130 -->
This configuration examines the **Si–You–Chou Metal assembly**, distinguishing the heavenly stem obtained with it.

**Yisi, Yiyou, and Yichou.** Yi uses Metal as Killings and welcomes Seal-Ribbon and restraint. Birth in the sixth month is unsuitable: Wei places flourishing Metal upon a Tomb, enabling it to control Wood. Birth in the eighth month with Killings exposed again is also unsuitable. Luck traveling Seal-Ribbon or flourishing Officer’s country can develop blessing.

**Dingsi, Dingyou, and Dingchou.** Ding uses Ren as Officer and welcomes flourishing Metal generating Water. The eighth month is unwelcome because Fire dies then, obstructing achievement. The eleventh month is also unwelcome because Gui Water becomes Killings and the lifespan cannot endure it. Flourishing Wealth and Officer in the pillars give distinction; a course through flourishing Officer develops blessing.

**Jisi, Jiyou, and Jichou.** Ji takes Jia Wood as Officer. Si–You–Chou Metal injures Officer and is also called Stealing Qi: how can this be auspicious? What is overlooked is that Metal can generate Water Wealth, so a Wealth course brings development. Bing, Ding, Yin, Wu [午], and Xu should not appear to injure the Metal configuration, nor should punishment, clash, breaking, or harm. Fourth-month birth is also unwelcome: flourishing Fire makes refined qi shallow, establishment comes late, and repeated success and failure, loneliness, and harm follow.

**Guisi, Guiyou, and Guichou.** Gui takes Metal Spirit as Seal. The Si–You–Chou Metal assembly generates Gui Water and welcomes autumn and winter. Fourth-month birth is nevertheless unwelcome because Water reaches Extinction at Si. Yet Metal is born at Si and generates Water, so Water cannot be wholly extinguished; Officer-and-Seal luck can develop blessing. Only Fire Wealth injuring Metal is disliked. ## 29. The Three Wonders’ True Nobility
### 三竒眞貴

<!-- source: L132-L134 -->
The classic says: “Meeting the Three Wonders’ true Lu and Horse spreads the name throughout the world. The day meets flourishing Lu and Horse, and one attempt brings fame.” A Jia day born in You, Chou, or Wei month, or a Yi day in Si or Shen month, with Ren, Gui, Hai, or Zi in the pillars, has Wealth, Officer, and Seal all complete: this is the nobility of the Three Wonders. Hurting Officer, Seven Killings, Rob Wealth, punishment, clash, breaking, and harm are avoided. Luck welcomes Wealth, Officer, Seal, and bodily strength. The person is intelligent, fond of learning, a prodigy and first examination graduate, principled and devoted to the Way and righteousness.[^69]

The Three Wonders have inner and outer forms. Openly exposed heavenly stems are outer; secretly concealed earthly branches are inner. Outer Wonders obtain wealth and distinction through labor, with some unease even at rest, because the heavenly stems are always moving. If Wealth and Officer decline in the monthly command, or luck travels declining ground, poverty follows. Bureau director **Zuo Jian**, **Jimao / Guiyou / Jiawu / Xinwei**, has Three Wonders among the heavenly stems. Yet the day stem declines, Wealth and Officer flourish, and Seal is weak: great distinction is unlikely.

Inner Three Wonders secretly assemble Wealth and Officer in the branches as True Lu and True Horse. Heavenly stems support and combine, no clash or control occurs, and the month communicates with the Birth and flourishing of Wealth and Officer. Xin born in Si month, for example, has Officer and Seal both at Established Lu; with flourishing Wealth and a vigorous Day Master, it merits the first rank. The classic says: “A man meeting the Three Wonders in Birth and flourishing must occupy the honor of the first rank.”

〈Grand Secretary **Zhang Juzheng**, **Yiyou / Xinsi / Xinyou / Xinmao**, has Officer and Seal at Established Lu in the monthly command. The Day Master sits upon Lu and flourishes in itself. Yi Wealth in the year draws into Mao hour, its Established Lu. Thus day ruler, Wealth, Officer, and Seal all sit upon Lu. Heavenly Virtue in the fourth month is Xin, while Xin and Yi exchange Returning Lu. Hence his appointment as chief minister while young, his rank as Junior Preceptor, six sons, and parents and brothers all preserved. His son and younger brother passed together in a Guiyou year; one son entered the Hanlin Academy. Among the court’s grand secretaries, none could rival his favor and trust. Minister **Tan Lun**, **Gengchen / Jiashen / Dingwei / Bingwu**, likewise has Wealth, Officer, and Seal in the monthly command. Dingwei is Eightfold Specialization and also returns to Lu in the hour. ## 30. The Heavenly Origin’s Hidden Lu
### 天元暗禄

<!-- source: L136-L138 -->
This configuration takes four days: **Gengyin, Yisi, Bingshen, and Jihai**. Geng takes Ding Fire as Direct Officer; without Ding in year, month, or hour, it apparently has no Officer. Geng’s own seat at Yin is welcome because Yin contains Fire’s Birth qi, which Geng controls and calls forth as Officer. Jia has Lu at Yin, and Wood is Fire’s mother: mother and child succeed one another. Wu or Ji in year, month, and hour should nourish the Heavenly Origin. Yi and Ding in the Four Pillars are finer still. Bing Killings requires Ren, Gui, Hai, or Zi to restrain it.[^74]

Yisi sits upon Metal at Birth as Officer and Wu’s Lu as Wealth. Further Geng and Wu drawn into the stems are welcome, with Ren and Gui Seal generating assistance. Xin Metal Seven Killings is avoided. Si originally contains flourishing Bing Fire; Ren, Gui, Hai, or Zi removing its Fire qi makes the arrangement beautiful. Bingshen welcomes Geng and Xin Wealth, Gui Water Officer, and Jia and Yi Seal; Wu and Ji injuring Officer are avoided. Jihai sits upon Jia Wood at Birth within Hai as Officer and avoids Metal injuring it.

The classic says: “Geng meeting Yin rejoices in Lu’s authority; Bing and Ding encountered must bring life to its end.” Another says: “Geng at Yin meeting Bing, with a flourishing ruler, suffers no danger.” Another: “Bing at Shen meeting yang Water can hardly preserve long life; Ji entering Hai’s palace and seeing yin Wood must finally lose longevity.” Again: “Yi entering Xun’s palace is called Not Extinguished.” These refer to the four days. Minister **Wen Yuan**, **Gengzi / Jiashen / Gengyin / Bingxu**; Minister **Pan Huang**, **Bingchen / Bingshen / Bingshen / Renchen**; a censor-in-chief, **Xinsi / Xinchou / Jihai / Bingyin**; a deputy commissioner, **Dinghai / Renyin / Jihai / Yihai**; and a county magistrate, **Jiazi / Dingmao / Yisi / Bingzi**, were all born on these days.

## 31. The Lu Origin’s Triple Assembly
### 禄元三㑹

A Jia day meeting Si–You–Chou welcomes Ren and Gui Seal generating assistance and avoids Hai–Mao–Wei clashing and controlling, Geng Killings, and Ding Hurting Officer. For a yang stem the assembly is impure; restraint in the pillars can permit Indirect Officer to be used. A Bing day meeting Shen–Zi–Chen welcomes Jia and Yi Seal and avoids Ren Killings, Ji Hurting Officer, and Yin–Wu–Xu clashing and controlling. A Wu day meeting Hai–Mao–Wei welcomes Bing and Ding Seal and avoids Jia Killings, Xin Hurting Officer, and Si–You–Chou clashing and controlling. A Geng day meeting Yin–Wu–Xu welcomes Wu and Ji Seal and avoids Bing Killings, Gui Hurting Officer, and Shen–Zi–Chen clashing and controlling. A Ren day meeting Yin–Wu–Xu welcomes Geng and Xin Seal and avoids Wu Killings, Yi Hurting Officer, and Shen–Zi–Chen clashing and controlling. The essential requirement is a strong body nourished by Seal. Luck in Officer, Seal, and bodily-strength territory must develop wealth and distinction.[^75]

Luoluzi’s account of Lu having three meetings instead means Jia meeting Yin together with Yin–Wu–Xu, or Yi meeting Mao together with Hai–Mao–Wei. This is also called Obtaining One and Dividing into Three: it refers to the Lu of Arrival at Office.

## 32. Exchanging the Lu Origins
### 禄元互換

<!-- source: L144-L146 -->
This configuration has only four days: **Wushen, Dingyou, Bingzi, and Gengzi**. Wushen day meets Yimao hour. Wu takes Yi Wood within Mao as Officer; Yi takes Geng Metal within Shen as Officer. Their exchange completes noble Lu. Ren and Gui in the pillars are welcome as Wealth generating and assisting Yi Officer. Luck reaching flourishing Officer’s country makes a noble fate. Jia Killings, Xin Hurting Officer, and the clashes of Yin or You are avoided. **Dingyou with Renyin, Bingzi with Guisi, and Gengzi with Dinghai** follow the same preferences and aversions. **Guihai / Renxu / Bingzi / Guisi** has flourishing Lu exchanged, each arriving at official nobility, without punishment, clash, breaking, or harm: hence distinction. **Jiwei / Xinwei / Bingzi / Guisi** also fits and is greatly noble. The older method’s Exchanged Lu Origins, such as Wuwu meeting Dingsi, instead takes the Lu of Arrival at Office.[^76]

## 33. The Six Ren Exchanging Positions
### 六壬移換

<!-- source: L148-L150 -->
In this configuration, Lu, Blade, Officer, or Seal exists within the pillars but does not serve the person directly. Clash and control cause transformation. Whether the conflict is among heavenly stems and earthly branches, among year, month, and day, or between day and hour as stem control and branch clash, the positions should exchange to become useful. Heavenly stems are always moving and earthly branches still; therefore the branches’ clash and control set the stems in motion. A Jiazi day at Gengwu hour, or Renzi day at Bingwu hour, is assessed through **Gengzi day with Jiawu hour**, or **Bingzi day with Renwu hour**, for good and ill. Gengwu day with Bingzi hour and Guihai day with Dingsi hour are interpreted as Bingwu and Dinghai respectively. Dingyou meeting Guimao alone does not exchange: Ding is born at You and Gui at Mao, each occupying Tianyi Noble Person and preferring its own generation.[^77]

The ancients’ Three-Fates method first established ruler and use: this was the profound meaning within absence. Blessing, calamity, wealth, and poverty are all fully recorded in their books. People now often apply them without obtaining a response because they do not understand the foregoing principle.

**Jisi / Guiyou / Dingmao / Guimao** has two Mao and one You, and Ding and Gui controlling one another. The Mao–You branch clash shakes the stems; Ding, flanked by two Gui, seems unable to take a step. But Ding is mother to Tai Sui’s Ji. Ji sees Gui controlling Ding, and the child comes to rescue the mother, injuring Guiyou Water instead. Gui avoids Ji and occupies Mao; Ding yields Mao to Gui and goes to its child’s place at You. Each finds noble ground: hence great distinction.

**Jiawu / Jiaxu / Wuchen / Renzi** has Jiaxu clashing with and controlling Wuchen. Wu seizes Ren’s place at Zi; Ren returns to Chen and becomes Ren Riding the Dragon’s Back. Wu obtains Zi, so day and hour both display the combined beauty of Wealth and Officer.

**Bingzi / Bingshen / Gengchen / Renwu** seems to have heavy Killings in the two Bing. Fortunately Renwu hour clashes with and controls Bingzi. Ren seizes Bing’s seat at Zi; Bing takes Geng’s seat at Chen; Geng avoids Bing and occupies Wu. Each has Officer and Seal complete. Moreover, in the seventh month Bing cannot control Ren Water, which becomes virtuous assistance strengthening Geng. This completes the subtlety of creative transformation.

**Yihai / Wuzi / Renwu / Wushen** places one Ren between two Wu, seemingly unable to survive. Fortunately the Zi–Wu clash sets things in motion. Wu takes Ren’s seat at Wu [午]; Ren avoids Wu 戊 and occupies Zi. Each holds Blade, their strengths equal, so they do not fight. The arrangement becomes **Wuwu–Wushen Arching Nobility**.

**Guihai / Dingsi / Jiazi / Gengwu** has Si–Hai and Zi–Wu clashes in the branches, setting the stems in motion too. Ding occupies Hai, Gui occupies Si, each reaching noble ground. Geng occupies Zi: although Death ground, it is near the monthly command’s Birth, and Bing Fire within Si is used as restrained Indirect Officer. Jia dies at Wu and is defeated at Zi, without support before or behind. Hence unforeseen calamity in later life.[^78]

## 45. Concealed Origins of Nobility and Killings
### 伏元貴煞

<!-- source: L196-L198 -->
For a Jia day, Officer is in You. Dingyou places Ding over Xin’s Lu and suppresses it. Killings is in Shen; Bingshen places Bing over Geng Killings and suppresses it. Wealth is in Wu [午] and Wei; Jiawu or Yiwei places Wood over Ji Earth, suppressing it so it dares not emerge. With Gengwu, Jia fears Geng and cannot approach Ji Wealth. Suppose Jia employs Xin Officer: Dingyou puts Officer beneath Hurting Officer; Xinsi puts Officer above Killings. If Fire has support and restrains Officer, Jia cannot obtain Xin Officer’s strength. Yet a month of flourishing Metal with Ren or Gui in the pillars to restrain or combine can still make blessing.[^88]

Bing is Food God, and its Lu is at Si. Water suppressing Bing denies Jia its Food; Bing in flourishing and assistance is auspicious. Geng is Killings, with Lu in Shen. Bingshen puts Geng beneath Bing’s restraint so it cannot rise to act as Killings. Killings transformed into authority can be employed for distinction. Wu and Ji are Wealth. Jiayin or Yimao in the pillars robs and restrains Wealth, preventing its acquisition; only Wealth communicating with the month’s Birth and flourishing makes it usable. Jiawu divides and seizes Wealth, yet two Wu [午] can develop it. Wuwu and Renwu indicate parental and ancestral Wealth. Gengwu is Wealth Generating Killings and is ominous. Bingwu gives wealth without distinction: dry Earth cannot generate Metal, and Officer is suppressed besides.

A poem says:
> Examine stems and branches for concealed noble origins and Killings;  
> Weigh good and ill with care.  
> Wealth and Officer uninjured, Killings unable to rise:  
> Blessing and Lu at birth are naturally extraordinary.

## 46. Eightfold Specialization with Flourishing Lu
### 八專禄旺

<!-- source: L200-L202 -->
The Eightfold-Specialization days were given earlier. Some accounts add **Bingwu, Dingsi, Wuchen, Wuwu, Jisi, Yichou, and Renzi**, but omit Dingwei. Among the sixty pairs, these alone have stems and branches of the same kind. Of them, **Jiayin, Yimao, Gengshen, and Xinyou** are the proper four days, each possessing its own exclusive flourishing Lu. Jia and Yi favor Hai, Mao, Wei, and Yin months to form a Wood configuration; Gengshen favors Si, You, Chou, and Shen months to form Metal. Their refined qi is pure and unmixed. They indicate intelligence, longevity, few illnesses, and frequent fondness for wine and sexual pleasure. Officer or Killings in the pillars is not feared when the body is strong; but mixing them prevents Lu from remaining exclusive and ultimately brings calamity.[^89]

Wealth, Seal, and Food among the Eight Characters make blessing. Courses through exclusive flourishing Lu or the flourishing ground of Wealth, Seal, and Food all develop wealth and distinction. Companions and Rob Wealth are feared. Without Wealth, Food, Officer, or Seal, solitude or the Buddhist or Daoist life is common. The classic says: “Stem and branch alike damage Wealth and injure the wife.” Again: “A flourishing body without support must become a Buddhist or Daoist.” A single Lu position, with luck strengthening the body and flourishing Wealth, Food, and Seal, also develops distinction. Three or four repeated Lu positions without Wealth or Officer can instead clash forth Wealth and blessing from the opposite palace. Or Officer in month or hour, possessing qi, makes a particularly noble configuration of a strong body meeting Officer. Clash and punishment dispersing one’s flourishing qi are avoided; even distinction then comes with frequent illness.

**Master Zhu, Duke Wen**, **Gengxu / Bingxu / Jiayin / Gengwu**, has exclusive Lu and a Fire configuration as Food. The two Geng are Killings, seated in Fire’s flourishing and Storehouse positions. Refined Wood obtains its ground and turns Killings into authority: he should become a great Confucian scholar. Chief Minister **Dong**, **Jisi / Xinwei / Yimao / Dinghai**, has exclusive Lu with a complete Wood configuration. The day stem gathers Birth, flourishing, and refined qi. Eastern luck strengthens the body and completes the configuration, clashing forth Lu from the opposite palace as authority: hence great distinction.[^90]

Another account calls **Dingwei, Jichou, Wuxu, and Wuchen** the four **Self-Holding** days; **Renzi, Guichou, Bingwu, and Dingsi** the four **Imperial-Prosperity** days. Wuwu and Jiwei also sit upon Imperial Prosperity. Thus Eightfold Specialization takes only the first four days. Examining the term, I suspect it should mean only eight days, as listed earlier. Another explanation derives it from the twenty-four directions: eight heavenly stems, twelve earthly branches, and Qian, Kun, Gen, and Xun. Only Zi, Wu, Mao, and You are exclusive; the others contain mixed qi and lack purity. This is its meaning.[^91]

## 47. Stems and Branches Sustaining Flourishing
### 干支持旺

<!-- source: L204-L206 -->
Jia and Yi days born in Hai, Mao, or Wei month meet Yin or Mao hour; Bing and Ding days in Yin, Wu, or Xu month meet Si or Wu hour; Wu and Ji days in Si or Wu month meet Chen, Xu, Chou, or Wei hour; Geng and Xin days in Si, You, or Chou month meet Shen or You hour; Ren and Gui days in Shen, Zi, or Chen month meet Hai or Zi hour. Ruler and root obtain ground, return to their proper direction, and enter a configuration, relying upon their own force for strength. Such a person is vigorous, seeks peaceful residence and distance from harm, withdraws from office and avoids rank, with modest fame and slight profit: one who leaves the dust and rises above the ordinary. Jia and Yi in Yin and Mao, or Bing and Ding in Si and Wu, are also called **Five Phases Unmixed**. The classic says: “The five tones unmixed have no disposition toward illicit desire.” Each occupies its Lu position, hence the saying.[^92]

## 48. Bending and Straightening
### 曲直

<!-- source: L208-L210 -->
A Jia or Yi day obtains a Hai–Mao–Wei assembly. Hai must be present, carrying Seal, for the configuration to form. Without Hai, Mao is merely Wood’s native qi; Metal and Earth are then needed for distinction. With neither Hai nor Metal and Earth, Wood has neither refinement nor substance, and distinction is difficult to assert. A Jiayin day with Hai also present but Dingmao in the hour has Rob Wealth, Yang Blade, and Hurting Officer: even if noble, it does not fit the configuration completely.[^93]

A poem says:
> Jia and Yi days obtaining Hai, Mao, and Wei  
> Complete Bending and Straightening and must gain splendid rank.  
> Without Hai in the pillars, Earth and Metal are suitable:  
> Such is the ground of blessing received at birth.

> Jia and Yi with Yin, Mao, and Chen  
> Are also called Benevolence and Longevity: weigh both.  
> Hai, Mao, and Wei complete dislike the White Emperor;  
> Kan’s position encountered must honor the person.

## 49. Blazing Upward
### 炎上

<!-- source: L212-L214 -->
A Bing or Ding day meets Yin–Wu–Xu. Yin carrying Seal must be present for the configuration to form. Without Yin, it is merely a fate among the nine vocations, close to the powerful. If Fire flourishes by itself without Hai Water to balance it, it is not noble. Northeastern luck is welcome. Chen, Chou, Wu 戊, and Ji obscuring Fire’s light are avoided; they often indicate eye disease or disorders of wind-qi. Wood in the pillars restraining them completes distinction. Water-and-Metal country and clashes are feared.[^94]

A poem says:
> Bing and Ding days seated upon Yin, Wu, and Xu:  
> From these arises Fire’s Blazing-Upward configuration.  
> Without Yin or Hai, fame does not form;  
> Earth obscuring it is avoided as bodily impairment.

## 50. Following Transformation
### 從革

<!-- source: L216-L218 -->
A Geng or Xin day meets Si–You–Chou. One or two Bing, Ding, Si, or Wu [午] positions must accompany it before it becomes a useful vessel; Fire must not be excessive. Xinsi, Xinyou, and Xinchou days dislike fifth-month birth, which allows Fire to injure them. The eighth month, or nourishment from Water and Earth as Food God and Seal-Ribbon, is auspicious.[^95]

A poem says:
> White Tiger meeting Si, You, and Chou  
> Is called Following Transformation, rich in reputation.  
> Meet Bing, Ding, Si, and Wu sparingly;  
> Noble qi forged to completion gives the most enduring office.

> Metal in Following Transformation wins noble regard;  
> Its nature is clear and lofty, its blessing deepest.  
> Fire arriving to mingle through the Four Pillars  
> Leaves monastic arts and empty worldly schemes.

## 51. Moistening Downward
### 潤下

<!-- source: L220-L222 -->
A Ren or Gui day meets the complete Shen–Zi–Chen assembly. Avoid drawing it into Mao and Si, the ground of Death and Extinction, or into the Three Punishments and four clashes. Death and Extinction prevent its flow; clash and punishment make it overflow. Years and courses follow the same rule. Some say that excessive Water requires one or two Earth influences in the pillars to restrain it and form banks. Once Earth is present, an assembly of Wood is ominous. Wood injuring Earth requires Metal Seal as rescue, but a lifetime of success and failure remains. Western luck is welcome; the southeast is unsuitable.[^96]

A poem says:
> Ren and Gui days meeting Shen, Zi, and Chen  
> Make the truest Moistening-Downward configuration.  
> Si and Wu, Chen and Xu, are nevertheless required;  
> Shen holding authority gives peerless distinction.

> Ren and Gui among the stems welcome winter birth;  
> Shen and Chen further complete the assembly.  
> Or all return to Hai, Zi, and Chou:  
> One walks level ground into the azure clouds.

## 52. Sowing and Reaping
### 稼穡

<!-- source: L224-L226 -->
〈The rhapsody says:〉 “Wu and Ji rejoice in the four seasonal endings: this is called Sowing and Reaping.” Wu or Ji born in a seasonal-ending month welcomes Wood as Officer; just one Wood is excellent. Much Wood weakens Earth and indicates deception, ruin of the household, and lack of benevolence. Chen and Wei are Earth’s gathering places. Si and Wu [午] Fire gives distinction but should not be excessive: too much dries Earth, preventing it from nourishing the myriad things. Chou and Xu contain Metal qi and should not recur heavily, lest killing qi remain and prevent life’s generation. Metal draining qi is also unsuitable and does not give distinction. Autumn Earth cannot become a useful vessel: it is dead Earth because it contains Metal. Winter Earth likewise fails: it is muddy Earth because it contains Water. Earth therefore belongs only to the four seasonal endings.[^97]

A poem says:
> Wu and Ji days suit the four seasonal endings;  
> Guard against Chou and Xu containing Metal qi.  
> Wood or bright Fire encountered at birth—  
> Within these changes lies true splendid rank.

> Wu and Ji born amid the four months  
> Require [Xu], Chen, Chou, and Wei complete.  
> Wealth ground is welcome, Officer and Killings disliked;  
> An eastern course must bring calamity.

## 53. An Earth Configuration in Moistening Downward
### 土局潤下

<!-- source: L228-L230 -->
〈The rhapsody says:〉 “Wu and Ji dwelling in Moistening Downward are duckweed and floating stems in a foreign land.” This image concerns **Wushen, Wuzi, and Wuchen** born in Shen–Zi–Chen or Ren–Gui Water’s flourishing country. Besides poverty and wandering, it indicates disorders of the limbs and eyes, or death from malignant sores, pus, and blood. The response comes in Chen, Xu, Chou, or Wei luck. Other weak Earth meeting a Water assembly likewise wanders. But self-flourishing Earth within a Water configuration is rich and noble, developing through Fire-country luck.[^98]

**Wuchen / Jiazi / Wushen / Yimao** is weak Earth meeting Moistening Downward, hence eye disease and military service. **Xinwei / Gengzi / Wuchen / Renzi** has Earth following abundant Water but also Earth to form banks. Bingshen and Dingyou courses generate Wealth and bring great development. On entering Yiwei luck, in a Wuwu year, wealth was lost, yet the person obtained the post of chief minister. One cannot uniformly call Wu and Ji in Moistening Downward ominous.

## 54. White Metal and Clear Water
### 金白水清

<!-- source: L232-L234 -->
〈The rhapsody says:〉 “White Metal and Clear Water: such people should succeed in the examinations.” This image is a **Gengshen or Xinyou day** born in autumn, drawn through the hour into Hai or Zi Water’s country. Metal is white; Water is clear. Without punishment, clash, breaking, or harm, blessing is substantial. Summer birth is strictly excluded. Spring Metal in the second or third month, traveling northwest, may also qualify. **Gengchen, Gengzi, Guisi, Guiyou, and Guichou** born in autumn or winter, without injuring Fire or restraining Earth, also qualify when Metal and Water balance and form an assembly.

A poem says:
> Clear Metal and white Water give splendid rank;  
> Elegant writing must surpass the crowd.  
> With no Fire or Earth to punish or restrain,  
> Reputation soars among the scholars of the academy.

## 55. Wood and Fire Illuminating One Another
### 木火交輝

<!-- source: L236-L238 -->
〈The rhapsody says:〉 “Bright Fire and refined Wood, born in spring, bring glory.” This image concerns **Jiaxu, Jiawu, Jiayin, Bingwu, Bingyin, and Bingxu** born in spring or summer, without Metal or Water injuring the pillars, with Wood or Fire in the hour and Wood-or-Fire luck. A Wood day with Fire’s refinement travels south; a Fire day with Wood’s refinement travels east: both give pure distinction and deep blessing. A Fire day with Fire’s refinement also becomes noble traveling east. Bingchen born in a month of flourishing Fire and traveling Wood and Fire may qualify, but gives wealth rather than rank. Refined Wood without Fire does not complete the configuration, for its basis is Wood and Fire shining through one another. **Dingsi / Jiachen / Jiayin / Dingmao**, **Jiawu / Bingyin / Dingmao / Bingwu**, and **Dingsi / Jiachen / Yisi / Dinghai** are all charts of Wood and Fire’s brilliance and pure distinction.[^99]

## 56. Fire and Metal Casting a Seal
### 火金鑄印

<!-- source: L240-L242 -->
〈The rhapsody says:〉 “Without Fire, Metal cannot become a vessel; without Metal, Fire cannot reveal its use.” Balanced Metal and Fire give the image of casting a seal. Chou is avoided as damage to the mold. The rhapsody says: “Riding the ceremonial carriage and wearing the court crown—how abundant are Metal and Fire!” Again: “Metal and its controlling ghost are without partiality.” This is the meaning.[^100]

## 57. Fire and Earth Intermixed
### 火土夾雜

<!-- source: L244-L246 -->
Fire meeting Earth grows dim; Fire lodged within Earth is obscured. Fire remaining Fire and Earth remaining Earth, neither covering the other, is excellent. Intermixed Fire and Earth indicate dullness and turbidity. The classic says: “Empty Fire and gathered Earth—what use can they serve? Surely a person of dusty, commonplace toil.” **Wushen / Jiwei / Bingwu / Yiwei**, **Gengxu / Jichou / Bingchen / Wuxu**, and **Wuxu / Dingsi / Jiwei / Bingyin** are all ordinary charts of intermixed Fire and Earth.

## 58. Azure and Red as Father and Son in the Hour
### 青赤時為父子

<!-- source: L248-L250 -->
The principle of azure and red in this image is the father transmitting the way to the son. People know that Wood generates Fire, Fire Earth, Earth Metal, Metal Water, and Water Wood, but do not understand yin generating yin and yang generating yang, or that yang producing yin is father and yin producing yang is mother. Ding combines with Ren and generates Jia and Ji. Ren takes Jia as Food and is Jia’s mother; Ding is Jia’s father. What generates me is mother. Ding takes Ji as Food; Ji takes Ren as father, and Ding takes Ji as mother: Water controls the mother.[^101]

Jia and Ji then combine. Jia takes Bing as Food; Ji takes Xin. With Jia in the day, Ren appears among the other three pillars. Bing and Xin combine again: Bing takes Wu as Food, Xin takes Gui. With Ren in the day, Jia appears among the other three, feeding forward through Wu and Gui. The cycle returns to its beginning. “Azure and Red as Father and Son in the Hour” means Yi Wood as ruler, meeting Yin, Wu, or Xu in the hour. This image indicates literary accomplishment.

## 59. Water and Earth Defeated at You
### 水土敗於酉

<!-- source: L252-L254 -->
This configuration is unfavorable in later life. It is particularly borne out when the natal fate is Water or Earth and the day ruler also meets Water or Earth. **Jiayin / Guiyou / Guiwei / Xinyou**, **Guihai / Yichou / Guiyou / Xinyou**, **Yimao / Bingyin / Jisi / Guiyou**, **Guiyou / Jiazi / Wuchen / Xinyou**, and **Xinyou / Jiawu / Wuzi / Xinyou** either held minor office and retired early, or remained ordinary and died early. Water and Earth’s Defeat at You is indeed unfavorable for the later years.[^102]

## 60. Flanking the Storehouse
### 夾庫

<!-- source: L256-L258 -->
〈An old song says:〉 “A Yisi birth welcomes Mao 〈flanking Chen〉; Guiyou at Hai hour must develop early 〈flanking Xu〉. Gengwu meeting Shen gives brocaded literary talent 〈flanking Wei〉; Bingzi at Yin hour brings abundant treasure 〈flanking Chou〉.” Filling the Storehouse position, punishment, clash, breaking, harm, and Void are greatly avoided. Years and courses welcome Officer and Seal. Day and month sharing a stem and arching an empty Storehouse are also favorable. **Yihai / Jimao / Jisi / Jiazi** has Ji as day ruler, seated in Si, the Imperial Seat. Jimao month secretly arches Water’s Storehouse within Chen as Wealth. Chen does not fill the pillars; Void, breaking, harm, punishment, and clash are absent. It is a chief minister’s fate. Infer the others accordingly.[^103]

## 61. Tomb Killings
### 墓煞

<!-- source: L260-L262 -->
An old song says:
> A ghost encountered in the Tomb must be understood;  
> Flanking Killings and holding the mound separates one’s kin.  
> This ominous star offended without rescue  
> Diminishes blessing and longevity in youth.

The cases are: Jia day meeting Gengxu or Gengchen; Yi meeting Xinchou or Xinwei; Bing meeting Renchen or Renxu; Ding meeting Guichou or Guiwei; Wu meeting Jiachen or Jiaxu; Ji meeting Yichou or Yiwei; Geng meeting Bingchen or Bingxu; Xin meeting Dingchou or Dingwei; Ren meeting Wuchen or Wuxu; Gui meeting Jichou or Jiwei. This is called **Seven Killings Entering the Tomb**. Luoluzi says: “Flanking Killings and holding the mound: family and relatives escort one in tears.” **Jisi / Wuchen / Guichou / Bingchen** has Gui taking Wu as Officer and Ji as Killings. Wu and Ji both occupy Chen, also Gui Water’s Storehouse, often indicating early development and early death. Another account says: Gui born in the fourth month with Wuchen hour has Officer Star Entering the Tomb, indicating early death and illness. The body lacks qi; Gui Water and Officer Star both enter Tomb ground and meet a ghost. Some say “Killings” here is not limited to Seven Killings: Yang Blade, Loss, and Robbery flanked and hidden in the Tomb by day–hour or day–month are all ominous.[^104]

## 62. Four Positions Pure and Complete
### 四位純全

<!-- source: L264-L266 -->
**Yin, Shen, Si, and Hai** contain the Five Phases’ generating qi, Traveling Horse, and Study Hall, and are called the **Four Births**. **Zi, Wu, Mao, and You** contain flourishing qi, with Yi, Xin, Ding, and Gui descending upon them, and are the **Four Cardinal Positions**. **Chen, Xu, Chou, and Wei** contain mixed qi, with Canopy and Direct Seal descending upon them, and are the **Four Tombs**.[^105]

The classic says: “Wealth, Officer, and Seal-Ribbon stand firmly in Yin, Shen, Si, and Hai.” The *True Treasure Rhapsody* says: “Zi, Wu, Mao, and You form the image of Eightfold Specialization: in civil life, a first-rank pillar supporting the state; in military life, enfeoffment and the commander’s seal complete achievement.” Again: “Yin, Shen, Si, and Hai reach the Three Excellencies.” The *Rhapsody on the Original Principle* says: “Zi, Wu, Mao, and You complete: lost in wine and desire.” The *Thousand-Li Horse* says: “The Four Storehouses complete: a transforming dragon encountering the great sea reaches the Nine-Five’s sovereign honor.” The *Precious Mirror Rhapsody* says: “Chen, Xu, Chou, and Wei in forward sequence are unquestionably an emperor’s fate.” The *Song of Ordering Folly* says: “The Four Storehouses complete in the hour are Four Noble Influences; one stands in the highest ranks and holds the balance.” *Walking Alone* says: “Yin, Shen, Si, and Hai are the Four-Birth configuration: with a useful influence and strong body, blessing develops. Zi, Wu, Mao, and You are the Four-Defeat configuration: a man encounters rise and fall, a woman solitude.”

Those fitting this configuration often have great wealth and distinction, but cannot avoid punishment and harm among the six relations and linked sequences of advance and retreat: the branches clash with one another without combination. If Zi, Wu, Mao, and You exchange positions through year, month, day, and hour, Zi takes Chen and Shen as kin; You takes Si and Chou as its kind; Wu takes Yin and Xu as partners; Mao takes Hai and Wei as connected branches. Each pillar favors its own relations, so the family must separate. Lonely Star, Widow Lodging, punishment, and harm make injury and misfortune heavier still. The *Essential Records* says: “Zi, Wu, Mao, and You in a valid configuration are the Four Extremities Complete; outside it they are Peach Blossom Filling the Fields. Men and women, even with rank and wealth, cannot avoid reckless wine and desire and thin virtue.”[^106]

Taiyi says: “What is excessively empty breaks. Like sudden wind and violent rain, it rises and declines easily. Conception must stand in to make a fate capable of enduring greatness.” Otherwise, I have seen many who offended clashes, rose early, and came to death.

A poem says:
> Yin, Shen, Si, and Hai in succession—  
> Chen, Xu, Chou, and Wei are likewise.  
> If one does not hold commanding power,  
> One must be an intimate attendant at court.

> Day Virtue, Day Nobility, and Kuigang,  
> Pure and complete in the Four Pillars, are greatly auspicious.  
> A configuration still forming within the six major forms—  
> How could such creative transformation be ordinary?

This takes four Day Virtues, four Kuigang, or four Day-Nobility positions, without other stems or branches, and judges them like Four Positions Pure and Complete.

## 63. Generated and Completed by One Qi
### 一氣生成

<!-- source: L268-L270 -->
〈This is the same as Four Positions Pure and Complete. Heavenly stems of one kind are **Heavenly Origin of One Qi**; a single branch repeated is **Earthly Things Alike**. The three configurations are discussed together.〉

Heavenly Origin of One Qi includes four **Renyin**, four **Xinmao**, four **Gengchen**, four **Jisi**, four **Wuwu**, four **Dingwei**, four **Bingshen**, four **Yiyou**, four **Jiaxu**, or four **Guihai**. Stems and branches throughout the Four Pillars share one qi, yet differences of weight, rank, and poverty require close distinction. Renyin, Xinmao, and Jiaxu give both wealth and distinction; Jisi also gives distinction. Wuwu and Dingwei have flourishing Blade and forceful natures: even noble, they have many dangers, harm wives, and do not end well. Gengchen gives rank and romantic freedom, a substantial name but slight profit. Yiyou often brings injury and disability; Guihai, poverty and meagerness. Bingshen born in the north can also attain distinction. Punishment, clash, breaking, and seizure in years or courses must bring calamity. Examine whether branches contain Wealth, Officer, Seal, or Food, whether a configuration forms without injury, whether the stems obtain the season, and whether these relations can transform or be followed. Such distinctions determine their degree and outcome.[^107]

Another account says: **four Wuwu** clash with Zi; Wu and Gui transform into True Fire, and Wu [午] combines with Yin and Xu as Seal: a fate of power and distinction. **Four Renyin** have Food God generating Wealth and secretly combine with Wu and Xu as Wealth, indicating great riches and rank. **Four Xinmao** secretly clash forth You Lu and give distinction, but later life has little wealth and insecure longevity. **Four Jiaxu** possess Wealth within Xu, making distinction. Yet all four Xu contain Fire and heavy Earth-Tomb qi, often giving solitude, loss of both parents in childhood, or many later calamities. You or Wei hour brings Hurting Officer Meeting Officer; Hai hour gives Rob Wealth and Blade and indicates poverty.[^108]

**Four Gengchen** clash forth Xu as noble Officer and are judged as Kuigang, though excessive Metal often makes cruelty and obstinacy. **Four Dingwei** have flourishing Blade, often making a fierce and wicked soldier; yet four Wei combine Hai to become **the Goat Striking the Pig’s Nobility**, raising someone from low service to great rank. Luck drawing Blade into combination and meeting Officer may suddenly bring calamity. Great vigilance is required: Blade configurations, especially carrying combination, all have this danger, which must be guarded against.

**Four Bingshen** have Fire in Illness and Death during the seventh month and flourishing Wealth generating Killings, an ominous condition. Fortunately the four Bing of one kind support the body and can control and gather Wealth, giving riches and rank or poverty followed by wealth. **Four Yiyou** have Conception-Origin Nobility: auspicious for men, but women often die violently and do not live long. **Four Guihai** have Water excessively flourishing and welcome clashing forth Bing and Wu within Si as Flying Heavenly Lu and Horse. Yet without You or Chou to hold Si by combination, only moderate distinction results. Filling the position is greatly avoided. **Four Jisi** indicate poverty and distress; or they may clash forth Ren and Jia within Hai as Wealth and Officer, while four Fires as Seal often make distinction. They are ominous for men and auspicious for women.[^109]

Another account says: four identical, unmixed stems make Heavenly Origin of One Qi and should not be judged simply as Companions. Examine the branches for generation, transformation, punishment, and control. With Wealth, Officer, or Seal forming a configuration, and years and courses not opposing it, great distinction must follow. Clash, punishment, and restraint are nevertheless ominous: do not insist that every One-Qi chart is noble. **Jiazi / Jiaxu / Jiayin / Jiazi**, for example, is also called the **Phoenix-Stem Configuration**. Bureau director **[Ye]**, **Renchen / Renzi / Renyin / Renyin**, has Ren reaching its own Extinction at Yin. This is mixed and uneven, hence his rank stopped at bureau director.[^110]

Another account says: four identical, unmixed branches make **Earthly Things Alike**, also called **Orchids and Angelica Flourishing Together**. Examine whether the stems cause blessing or calamity to gather in those branches. Gathered blessing fitting a configuration often gives the distinction of the two chief councils. **Jiayin / Bingyin / Gengyin / Wuyin**, for example, is also called the **Phoenix-Branch Configuration**. Three Zi with Hai hour are **Rats Roaming by Night**; three You with Yin hour, **Roosters Announcing Dawn**; Chou meeting Jiwei, **Rhinoceros Gazing at the Moon**; Yin meeting Jisi, **Fierce Tiger Roaring into the Wind**. All can be inferred through principle: their living application rests with the reader.[^111]

*Walking Alone* says: “Heavenly Origin of One Qi, Earthly Things Alike: a fate obtaining these stands among the Three Excellencies.” A poem says:
> Heavenly Origin of One Qi assures honor and glory;  
> The stems unmixed, one character remains pure.  
> It cannot be judged as Companions contending:

> One heavenly character: Fire warm and flowing.

> Fourfold yang Water and fourfold Yin:

## 76. Auspicious and Inauspicious Assemblies

An inauspicious assembly means the fate avoids Seven Killings, Hurting Officer, Yang Blade, Loss, or Robbery, yet major course, minor course, and flowing year all assemble them: great calamity must follow. ## 79. Combination and Transformation Forming a Configuration
### 合化成局

<!-- source: L332-L334 -->
Combination means harmony; transformation means change, as in Jia combining with Ji. Jia’s native image is Wood, but combining with Ji transforms it into Earth; it is no longer judged as Wood. The classic says: “The Heavenly Origin’s transforming qi is originally true, yet no one in the world fully brings its meaning to light. Ding and Ren transforming Wood find Jiachen alone a blessing: who knows that this is Chief Star? Whoever meets it must enjoy great fame. Wu and Gui transforming Fire later meet Bingchen: this is the subtle principle. The Three Origins move among one another with little obstruction; the person lets the ceremonial sash fall and enters the court. These are Heaven’s true workings in creation: gold and white jade cannot buy them. With Birth, flourishing, and Storehouse giving support, wealth, rank, and achievement fill the world.”[^127]

Yet a configuration must form and flourishing qi must gather in the hour; obtaining the month’s qi as well is particularly fine. Sometimes the day’s own flourishing suffices for transformation. Stems and branches may also transform of themselves, but luck must then travel the transformed qi’s flourishing country or the arrangement is useless. The month’s qi alone does not suffice for transformation. When the day possesses this qi, disregard the ordinary Wealth, Officer, Seal, and Food categories: Birth and flourishing completing a configuration, followed by luck in transformed qi’s flourishing country without injuring control, all lead to development. A self-flourishing body, or generating qi in the pillars strengthening it, gives the Transformation Image greater power. Heavy mutual control preventing the image is greatly avoided and indicates poverty; clashing and controlling luck also brings much calamity and death. If stems combine but do not form a configuration, they do not transform: judge good and ill through the original stems and branches without invoking transformed qi.[^128]

Wu follows Gui in combination and completes transformation into Fire. Yi ultimately gives distinction; Ren also brings abundance. Sons gathering in support welcome Ding at Yi’s position; disharmony among the six relations arises from Jia flourishing in Yin’s palace. Blazing Bing Fire makes blessing and Lu hard to find; glittering Geng Metal readily gives success. Injury to wife and children comes from flourishing Ji; clumsy plans from powerful Xin.

### Tenth: Gui Combining with Wu
#### 其十
<!-- source: L372-L374 -->
Gui follows Wu in combination, transforming into Fire as it descends. Xin concealed within Bing gives a lifetime of repeated success and failure; Ji hidden within Jia, a hundred years of labor in body and mind. Full granaries welcome Ding Fire; substantial fields and wealth delight in Geng Metal. Repeated Yi advances office and honor; Ren above and below brings property, wealth, and rank. Wealth’s gains and losses arise from excessive Xin Metal; a faltering official career from Ji Earth’s intrusion.

## 80. The Returning Image
### 返象

<!-- source: L376-L378 -->
**Returning** means meeting Birth in Extinction. Yi and Geng transforming Metal, for example, are born in Yin month, Metal’s Extinction ground. Heavy Wood in the pillars nevertheless attempts to transform into Metal; the transformed qi loses its configuration, hence a Returning Image. The *Jade Well* also calls a return to the root within transformation, or reversed transformation, “returning.” Yi and Geng with many Hai and heavy Wood transform not into Metal but back into Wood. The meaning is the same. Years and courses are judged accordingly. A weak body meeting Officer or Killings may also be called Returning: Officer brings advance, Killings retreat.[^132]

The classic says: “Transformation’s product dwells in Decline, Tomb, and Extinction; its image forms a mixed configuration, meeting combination as though not meeting it.” The person’s disposition follows the image: frequent changes of residence, reversals without establishment, an unsettled mind. The interpretation of a weak body meeting Officer and Killings has the same meaning. **Xinwei / Renchen / Bingwu / Guisi** takes Gui as Bing’s Officer, but Ren comes to control. Bing and Xin transform into Water, while Chen, Si, Wu, and Wei in the pillars are Water’s Tomb, Decline, and Extinction ground: a true Returning Image. **Guichou / Bingchen / Bingshen / Xinmao** has Bing combining with Xin. Bing Fire seated in Shen has no qi; Xin Metal seated in Mao declines. In flourishing Xin-Metal luck, the husband follows the wife; in flourishing Bing-Fire luck, the wife follows the husband. This fate has no ancestral estate and a life of advance and retreat without accomplishment. Infer the other cases accordingly.

## 81. The Illuminating Image
### 照象

<!-- source: L380-L382 -->
**Illumination** means the lofty brightness of Fire and Earth. Fire’s qi is high and bright; Earth governs sowing and reaping. Earth above is like haze and mist covering the sky; Fire below, like sunlight piercing through. This is darkness first, brightness afterward. A Wu day obtaining a Yin–Wu–Xu Fire assembly, or Wu [午] Fire in the branches with Bing in the hour stem generating Wu Earth, is called an Illuminating Image. Water should not appear in the pillars: it dissolves Earth and extinguishes Fire, diminishing blessing. Fire above and Water below is also an Illuminating Image, like the sun shining in the sky and giving light beneath the water; the light can reflect upward too.[^133]

The classic says: “Four Pillars uninjured lead directly above the ranks of court; even apprehension within the branches must bring reputation rather than poverty. Luck reaching declining country must bring calamity.” **Wuxu / Wuwu / Bingwu / Wuxu** gives Bing day three Wu as Food, with flourishing Fire generating them. The Four Pillars are uninjured; Wu and Xu can also clash forth Officer Star in Zi and Chen. **Bingxu / Guisi / Wuwu / Dingsi** has Wu Earth as day stem and a pure Si–Wu–Xu Fire assembly below. Bing and Ding in year and hour generate Wu. Although one Gui occurs, it also transforms into Fire; transformed qi obtains both season and position. Both therefore give great distinction. **Wuxu / Jiayin / Bingwu / Jiawu** has four uninjured positions; **Jiaxu / Bingyin / Bingwu / Gengyin** has illumination above without breaking. Both are chief ministers’ fates.

## 82. The Ghost Image
### 鬼象

<!-- source: L384-L386 -->
A **ghost** is Killings: the stem meets its controlling Killings. Distinguish upper and lower stems and branches, whether ghost flourishes and body declines or body flourishes and ghost declines. Yi Wood, for example, takes Geng Metal as Officer and combines in the stems; further Xinyou Seven Killings makes the ghost flourish. The classic says: “When one’s body faces a ghost, distinguish Heaven and Earth. The image’s flourishing or decline determines glory, withering, rank, and poverty. A declining body and flourishing ghost should bring bodily injury; a flourishing body and declining ghost must make a violent person. Ghost and body both declining make a wandering man or a Buddhist nun.”[^134]

The *Jade Well* says: “Body and ghost both strong: military methods and penal administration may come to use. Body and ghost both weak: what doubt of failure, ruin, and reckless wandering?” Dominant Wood employs slight Metal; abundant Water relies upon ill Earth; flourishing Metal relies upon declining Fire; strong Fire borrows shallow Water; thick Earth seeks dead Wood. A body riding flourishing and meeting the Ghost Image can instead make a noble fate: restraint within strength completes blessing. Body and ghost both manifest, with Blade providing restraint, give fierce courage and high distinction; sometimes office is gained through wine. **Guimao / Xinyou / Yihai / Xinsi** has Yi Wood without qi in the eighth month and two flourishing Xin Killings, hence disability. **Wuchen / Jihai / Jiashen / Gengwu** sits upon Killings and also meets Geng hour. The body is controlled; Wood flourishes within Hai but dies at Wu. This is a wandering fate.

## 83. The Hidden Image
### 伏象

<!-- source: L388-L390 -->
**Hidden** means concealed and unmanifest. Wealth, Officer, Seal, and Killings in the pillars do not communicate with the month’s qi and have not emerged into the stems; they are hidden within the branches’ human origins, without visible form and difficult to discern. The *Jade Well* says: “The body does not communicate with the month’s qi, but conceals its own qi in another branch and can rise with assistance.” A day stem meeting neither Birth nor flourishing, with much Death and Extinction, and excessive Officer or Killings suppressing the body, is also hidden. The classic says: “Officer and ghost both complete may give long life without fulfillment. Damage in the stems lies within the branches; arts and crafts accompany the person.”[^135]

**Wuwu / Jiayin / Wuyin / Xinyou** has Wu day controlled excessively by Jiayin. The body has no qi, hence a craftsman. **Guichou / Xinyou / Jiazi / Jisi** has Jia Wood without qi in the eighth month, an excessively strong monthly Officer, and the Si–You–Chou Officer assembly. Nobility may exist but cannot be enjoyed: success on the secondary examination list, or death upon taking office. Generally, hidden qi without assistance means isolation, poverty, and short life; assistance changes the judgment.

## 84. The Image of Belonging
### 屬象

<!-- source: L392-L394 -->
**Belonging** means each of the Five Phases’ affiliations: the region occupied by the governing stem and its eastern, western, southern, or northern influence. Yin–Mao–Chen belong to eastern Wood; Si–Wu–Wei to southern Fire; Shen–You–Xu to western Metal; Hai–Zi–Chou to northern Water. Each exclusively holds one region’s qi. The classic says: “First distinguish south and north, east and west; next distinguish within the triple combinations.” Then examine whether the heavenly stem suits the region to which it belongs. Wealth, Officer, Seal, Food, or Noble Person may complete a noble image. A stem enters that region and responds to its kindred qi, just as an orange planted south of the river is an orange but transplanted north becomes a bitter orange. Each suits the qi to which it belongs—how could this be a person’s clever contrivance?[^136]

With the Yin–Mao–Chen Wood image, Wu and Ji Earth stems are judged as belonging to Wood. With Si–Wu–Wei Fire, Geng and Xin Metal belong to Fire; with Shen–You–Xu Metal, Jia and Yi Wood belong to Metal; with Hai–Zi–Chou Water, Bing and Ding Fire belong to Water. Without qi there is no such judgment; with qi there is belonging. **Renzi / Renzi / Guichou / Guichou** has Zi and Chou belonging to the north. Ren and Gui divide and seize the two Zi, Gui’s Lu. The day stem is too strong. Gui takes Fire as wife and Earth as child: Water extinguishes Fire and dissolves Earth, leaving both wife and child weak. Hence a Buddhist or Daoist life, or at least childlessness. **Yiyou / Jiashen / Yiyou / Yiyou** has Jia and Yi Wood belonging to Shen and You below as Officer: this is Censor-in-chief **Liu, Duke Wenzhuang**. **Yiyou / Jiashen / Jiashen / Jiaxu** likewise gives Jia and Yi the beauty of Wealth and Officer through the branches’ Shen–You–Xu Metal, completing a noble image.

## 85. The Image of Kind
### 類象

<!-- source: L396-L398 -->
**Kind** means assembling one household. Jia and Yi obtaining Hai–Mao–Wei form Wood; Bing and Ding obtaining Yin–Wu–Xu form Fire; Geng and Xin obtaining Si–You–Chou form Metal; Ren and Gui obtaining Shen–Zi–Chen form Water; Wu and Ji obtaining Chen–Xu–Chou–Wei form Earth. The Five Phases follow the weightiest influence as their kind. Another account compares this with Companions: all phases return to a single purity, though stems of one kind do not inherently make the image. A resemblance to transformed qi without a completed configuration, or to Seal-Ribbon without actual Seal, produces poverty, obstruction, marriage into another household, or adoption elsewhere.[^137]

**Bingyin / Wuxu / Gengxu / Wuyin** has two Wu generating Geng, resembling Seal. Two Yin control Wu as Seven Killings; Yin–Wu–Xu Fire with exposed Bing in the year seems like Seal but is not Seal. Scorching is excessive, and the person relies upon parents in another household. **Dingwei / Jiyou / Yisi / Yiyou** has Yi Wood meeting a Si–You–Chou Metal configuration, seeming to follow Metal’s transformation. But without Geng it cannot transform and instead becomes Killings. The person is forceful, frequently ill, dependent, poor, and short-lived. The classic says: “Generation that does not generate makes one adopted or marrying in; transformation that does not transform makes one stumble and remain delayed.”

**Gengxu / Wuyin / Gengwu / Bingxu** has Metal resembling Fire, turbid rather than pure. **Dingchou / Bingwu / Gengyin / Bingxu** also has Metal resembling Fire: noble, but not enduring. **Guimao / Yimao / Jimao / Yihai** and **Yihai / Jimao / Jimao / Yihai** have Ji Earth resembling Wood. **Yisi / Yiyou / Yiyou / Yiyou** has Yi resembling Metal and does not fear many ghosts. These latter charts all have blessing and distinction.

## 86. The Following Image
### 從象

<!-- source: L400-L404 -->
**Following** means husband and wife following one another. Examine the qi drawn into use: when the husband flourishes, the wife follows; when the wife flourishes, the husband follows. Officer and Killings are husband; Wealth is wife. The names of spouses are borrowed to judge a person’s good and ill. A Yi day born in Si, You, Chou, or Shen is wife following husband; a Geng day in Hai, Mao, Wei, or Yin is husband following wife. If following occurs, judge by the branches’ exclusive qi. If it returns to its root, judge from that root. The *Jade Well* says: “Follow whatever possesses qi; a numerous party may also be followed.” Again: “The self without qi follows the configuration and changes image: this corresponds to the branches’ triple assembly.” The classic says: “Following contains rank and poverty. Manifest rank in Following obtains the season and reaches the Three Excellencies; decline and failure in Following lose the season and bring solitude, poverty, and wandering.”

**Jiaxu / Dingmao / Gengshen / Jimao** has Gengshen Metal flourishing in itself, yet Mao month and Mao hour make Wood extremely strong. The wife flourishes and the husband follows, indicating wealth through the wife or by joining her household. **Gengshen / Wuyin / Jiyou / Bingyin** has Ji day with two Yin in month and hour, whose Jia is Direct Officer: the husband flourishes and the wife follows. Although [Gengshen] seeks to injure Yin, Bing Fire at Birth within Yin restrains it. Jia uses Xin Metal within You as Direct Officer; the husband-star obtains Officer, and Jia’s Lu returns to Yin. Thus the woman marries a distinguished husband of the Three Terraces.[^138]

Another account says rootlessness makes a Following Image. **Yiyou / Renwu / Jiashen / Jisi** has Jia Wood without support and can follow Earth. **Gengchen / Yiyou / Yiyou / Gengchen** and **Gengxu / Yiyou / Yiyou / Renwu** both have Yi Wood without support, following Metal and transforming. Gengchen and Gengxu also have Metal nayin: both are noble fates. *Walking Alone* says: “Abandoning the fate to follow Wealth requires Wealth to assemble; abandoning it to follow Killings requires Killings to assemble. Following Killings welcomes Wealth; following Wealth avoids Killings. If the following condition meets its own rooted qi, the fate is certainly harmed.”[^139]

## 87. The Transforming Image
### 化象

<!-- source: L406-L408 -->
**Transformation** means yin and yang combining and transforming, Heaven and Earth balancing, and the Five Phases matching evenly. The classic says: “A configuration completed within transformation turns with luck and receives enfeoffment beside the emperor.” Again: “A hidden Transformation Image gives a commonplace life.” Balanced stems and branches transform; examine whether day and hour combine and transform. The *Jade Well* says: “The five qi have transforming images, which must be singly pure and clean. Transformation returning may be noble or lowly; transformation that fails may give long life or early death.” The *Discussion of Penetrating Mystery* says: “Yi flourishing makes Geng follow; Geng flourishing makes Yi follow. Only when the Geng Day Master lacks qi and the transformed state possesses qi can it be used. If neither has qi, it cannot.” Ding and Ren transforming Wood in spring make the husband follow the wife’s generation; in winter, the wife follows the husband’s generation. This is transformation.[^140]

**Jiayin / Jisi / Jiaxu / Renshen** is Jia–Ji transforming Earth, obtaining Earth’s configuration and Birth at Shen. **Jiaxu / Jiayin / Guisi / Wuwu** is Wu–Gui transforming Fire, born in Yin month, arriving at office in Si and flourishing at Wu: transformed qi obtains season and position. **Xinmao / Wuxu / Jiayin / Gengwu** has Jia approaching Ji Earth within Wu to transform into True Earth, giving deep blessing, wealth, and rank. Infer the rest accordingly.

The Daoist **Xuanxu** says: “For Kind, Belonging, Following, and Transformation, distinguish strength and decline; for Illumination, Concealment, Arching, and Reaching Afar, distinguish manifest and hidden.” The Layman of **Jingshan** explains: “Jia or Yi day with Yin–Mao–Chen complete is Kind; with Hai–Mao–Wei complete, Belonging. Yi day with Si–You–Chou or Shen–You–Xu is Following; Jia seeing Ji, or Yi seeing Geng, is Transformation. Kind and Belonging need a flourishing body; Following and Transformation need decline. Bing or Ding with Fire throughout the Four Pillars and Mao Wood in the hour is Wood and Fire Illuminating One Another. Ren or Gui with Water throughout and one Metal in the hour is Metal and Water Illuminating One Another. Ren born in Wu month, with rootless Water, abandons the fate to follow Ding Fire within Wu. Ding and Ren combine like spouses: this is a Hidden Image.” These definitions of Concealment and Illumination differ, but their principles do not differ in their capacity for distinction. The eight methods above are great keys to establishing a fate’s configuration. Examine them carefully, then consider the various authorities’ noble configurations.[^141]

## 88. Heaven and Earth Combining in Virtue
### 天地德合

<!-- source: L410-L412 -->
〈What follows is the *Guide’s* account of proper nobility.〉 Stems are Heaven’s clear qi; branches are Earth’s substantial support. Stem combination wins the hearts of worthy people: what is rooted in Heaven draws close to what is above. Branch combination wins the hearts of the many: what is rooted in Earth draws close to what is below. Stems and branches both combining make **Heaven and Earth Combining in Virtue**, as Jiazi meeting Jichou or Wuxu meeting Guimao. Combination in the hour is best; in the day, next. Year combining with month and day with hour makes blessing especially immediate. Ministers **Zhang Shunchen**, **Yimao / Dinghai / Wuyin / Guihai**, and **Lin Jiansu**, **Xinwei / Dingyou / Jimao / Jiaxu**, are examples.[^142]

## 89. Ruler and Minister Meeting in Celebration
### 君臣慶㑹

<!-- source: L414-L416 -->
The stem images the ruler, the branch the minister. Stems and branches combining within one ten-day round are **Ruler and Minister Meeting in Celebration**: Jiaxu meeting Jimao, or Wuchen meeting Guiyou. Combination in the hour is best; in the day, next. Year with month and day with hour make **Paired Mandarin Ducks Combining in Virtue**, finer still. These two configurations were already discussed under combination and transformation, though not separately named as configurations.[^143]

An old song says:
> A Jia person: Jisi reaches Jiashen;  
> Yi finds Gengchen close to Yiyou.  
> Bing requires Xinmao and Bingxu;  
> Beneath Ding, Renyin rides with Dinghai.  
> Wu birth finds Guihai and Wuyin day;  
> Xin welcomes Bingshen and Xinsi.  
> Ren meets Dingwei together with Renwu;  
> Gui attends Wuwu and Guiwei’s spirit.  
> Ji brings Jiaxu to Jimao:  
> These give splendor, blessing, and long life.  
> If other configurations join them,  
> A king, marquis, or chief minister is assured.

Minister **Yu**, **Guiwei / Jiayin / Wuwu / Bingchen**; Academician **Cai**, **Jiashen / Wuchen / Jisi / Gengwu**; Chief Minister **Wei**, **Jimao / Jiaxu / Yiyou / Gengchen**; and Minister **Xie Yuanming**, **Dingsi / Jiyou / Jiashen / Renshen**, are examples.

## 90. One Qi as the Root
### 一氣為根

<!-- source: L418-L420 -->
The nayin of year, month, day, hour, and conception is pure Metal, Wood, Water, Fire, or Earth. **Jiazi / Guiyou / Xinsi / Yiwei**, with **Jiazi conception**, is an example. The rhapsody says: “One Qi as the Root makes a regional inspector or an officer of the personnel ministry.” A Wood person obtaining Yin–Mao–Chen, Fire obtaining Si–Wu–Wei, Metal obtaining Shen–You–Xu, or Water obtaining Hai–Zi–Chou also has one qi. Such a configuration does not dislike many ghosts; many make distinction. One qi throughout stems and branches—as Jia or Yi obtaining Ding–Ren and complete Hai–Mao–Wei—is finer still. Censor-in-chief **Ling Xiang**, **Yiwei / Gengchen / Renyin / Xinhai**; first examination graduate **Wang Yikui**, **Yisi / Wuzi / Bingshen / Dingyou**; and Minister **Weng Wanda**, **Wuwu / Jiwei / Guisi / Dingsi**, are examples.[^144]

## 91. Two Stems Unmixed
### 兩干不雜

<!-- source: L422-L424 -->
Year, month, day, and hour successively occupy two stems, pure and unmixed. **Jiazi / Yihai / Jiaxu / Yichou** has Jia and Yi without disorder; **Bingyin / Dingyou / Bingchen / Dingyou** has Bing and Ding without disorder. The rhapsody says: “Similar kinds at the stems: the smell of copper and lowly office.” Jia receiving Yi or Yi receiving Jia is called Indirect Lu and often lacks examination fame.[^145]

## 92. A Gathering of Threefold Accord
### 三合聚集

<!-- source: L426-L428 -->
〈Also called the **Dragon-and-Phoenix Three-Terrace Configuration**.〉 Within year, month, day, hour, and conception, three stem positions, three branch positions, or three nayin positions may be alike. It takes the fullness of number in “One generates two, two generates three, and three generates the myriad things.” Three Ding and one Gui, three Ren and one [Wu], or three Geng and one Bing are examples. Excessive Established Lu or flourishing is strictly avoided, especially further excess within it. Earth and Metal are exceptions: three Metals obtaining Earth, three Earths obtaining Fire, or three Wu, Geng, or Xin do not suffer from Established Lu and flourishing. Branch examples are three Yin and one Shen as Horse, or three Hai and one Yin as combination. Three nayin positions, such as **Jiazi, Yichou, and Renshen**, give Metal obtaining ground and are then auspicious.[^146]

## 93. The Five Phases All Complete
### 五行俱足

<!-- source: L430-L432 -->
Year, month, day, hour, and conception carry Metal, Wood, Water, Fire, and Earth, whether in true qi or nayin, with qi in the Three Origins. This is also **Meeting All Five Storehouses**. **Jiazi / Wuchen / Dingsi / Dingwei**, with **Jiwei conception**, is an example. They must generate one another, not be in Death or Extinction; blessing spirits must ride in mutual succession for distinction. Self-Death or self-Extinction without rescue prevents the configuration. Huzhongzi says: “The Five Phases All Complete does not count conception, but takes the true qi at birth to make the circle whole. True qi is the ruler of that year; those who add a conception-month nayin position misunderstand.” Ministers **Li Tingxiang**, **Xinchou / Jiawu / Bingshen / Wuxu**, with **Yiyou conception**, and Hanlin academician **He Luowen**, **Bingshen / Xinchou / Renzi / Xinhai**, with **Renchen conception**, are examples.[^147]

## 94. Six Positions Riding One Another
### 六位相乘

<!-- source: L434-L436 -->
Add the year’s or day’s Lu and Horse, or the Life Palace, to year, month, day, hour, and conception. They combine to evoke the twelve branch spirits; the heavenly stems of the five positions also combine to complete all ten stems, making distinction. **Jiazi / Dingmao / Wuyin / Xinyou**, with **Wuwu conception** and Life established at Si, is an example. My fate, **Renwu / Guichou / Gengyin / Bingxu**, with **Jiachen conception** and Lu and Horse in Shen, is another. 〈Yuwu records this himself.〉[^148]

## 95. Gathering Essence and Uniting Spirit
### 聚精㑹神

<!-- source: L438-L440 -->
The *Huainanzi* says: “Essence and spirit are received from Heaven; bodily form is endowed by Earth.” Li is Fire, Kan Water. Fire is the sun and Water the moon; sun and moon travel through Heaven to complete the four seasons. These are Heaven’s essence and spirit. Water is essence; Fire spirit. Essence is qi’s mother, qi is spirit’s child. One qi circulates, giving enduring life without death: these are the human being’s essence and spirit. A fate with two Waters and two Fires in stems, branches, or nayin, each riding flourishing and obtaining ground, unmixed with other things, combining and completing one another, has vital brilliance moving together with Heaven. Not only does distinction surpass others, but life is long. Grand Secretaries **Li Dongyang**, **Dingmao / Dingwei / Guihai / Jiwei**, and **Yang Yiqing**, **Jiaxu / Bingzi / Renwu / Dingwei**, are examples.[^149]

## 96. Spirits Concealed and Killings Submerged
### 神藏煞沒

<!-- source: L442-L444 -->
Jia, Geng, Bing, and Ren are the yang stems’ auspicious meeting, especially fine when born in a season’s first month. Yi, Xin, Ding, and Gui are the yin stems’ noble virtues, finest in a season’s final month. The six ominous spirits hide here; the four evil sha are submerged. Four clearly distinguished stems in year, month, day, and hour constitute auspicious qi regardless of punishment, harm, clash, and breaking; Lu, Horse, Officer, and Seal make it more auspicious. The rhapsody says: “Yang’s oddness and yin’s pairing make the greatest heroes.”[^150]

Some give four Five-Phase Tomb Sha. In Yin–Wu–Xu months, sha lies at Chou, Metal’s Tomb, so Great Auspiciousness is the sha; at Qian it submerges. In Hai–Mao–Wei months, sha lies at Xu, Fire’s Tomb: River Chief is Fire Sha, submerging at Kun. In Shen–Zi–Chen months, sha lies at Wei, Wood’s Tomb: Lesser Auspiciousness is Wood Sha, submerging at Xun. In Si–You–Chou months, sha lies at Chen, Water’s Tomb: Heavenly Gang is Water Sha, submerging at Gen. Thus someone born in Yin, Wu, or Xu month, with Chou day and Hai hour, has the sha submerged.

Tianyi has six ominous generals. Soaring Snake and Vermilion Bird belong to Fire: at Hai, Ren, Zi, or Gui they fall into water or cast themselves into the river. White Tiger belongs to Metal: at Si, Bing, Wu, or Ding its body burns. Dark Warrior belongs to Water: at Kun, Gen, Chen, Xu, Chou, or Wei its feet break. Restraint and control overcome them: this is the six spirits concealed, the sha such as Dark Warrior Holding Authority.[^151]

## 97. Lu and Horse Galloping across One Another
### 禄馬交馳

<!-- source: L446-L448 -->
For Yin, Wu, and Xu, Horse is in Shen and the hour stem obtains Geng; for Hai, Mao, and Wei, Horse is in Si and the hour stem obtains Bing or Wu; for Shen, Zi, and Chen, Horse is in Yin and the hour stem obtains Jia; for Si, You, and Chou, Horse is in Hai and the hour stem obtains Ren. These may be exchanged among the stems and branches of year, month, day, and hour. If absent from year and month but mutually present in day and hour, they are particularly fine. Clash, breaking, and Void are avoided.

## 98. Driving Lu Forward and Reining Horse In
### 趕禄欄馬

<!-- source: L450-L452 -->
For a Jiaxu birth, Jia’s Lu is in Yin: Dingchou drives it forward. Xu’s Horse is in Shen: Guiyou reins it in. One need not seek Lu and Horse in their actual positions; they come of themselves. This configuration is supremely noble. As the saying goes: “Lu does not develop unless driven forward; Horse does not stay unless reined in.”

## 99. Gathering and Unfolding Blessing
### 集福發福
<!-- source: L454-L456 -->
The four positions gather blessing at the Imperial Seat, and the Imperial Seat unfolds blessing through the four positions. This was discussed earlier under year, month, day, and hour. Vice Minister **Wang Bing**, **Renzi / Renzi / Yiyou / Dingchou**, and Censor-in-chief **Wu Yuan**, **Wuyin / Jiayin / Renyin / Wushen**, are examples.

## 100. Stems and Branches Doubly Linked
### 干支䨇連
<!-- source: L458-L460 -->
Jiawu meeting Yiwei, Bingshen meeting Dingyou, or Wuxu meeting Jihai links upper and lower positions in succession. Jiazi meeting Yichou, or Gengwu meeting Xinwei, has Jia and Geng’s Noble Person at Chou and Wei, and Yi and Xin’s at Zi and Wu. This is also **The Two Modes’ Noble Pairing**. Such pearl-linked blessing spirits can suppress many sha; Lu and Horse added make a fully endowed fate. Pearl-linking can advance or retreat: Bingyin meeting Dingmao shares one qi but retreats in qi; Dingmao meeting Bingyin moves back one position yet advances, giving true qi.[^152]

## 101. Heavenly Stems Strung like Pearls
### 天干連珠
<!-- source: L462-L464 -->
Year, month, day, hour, and conception obtain **Jia–Yi–Bing–Ding–Wu** or **Ji–Geng–Xin–Ren–Gui**. Jia combines Ji, Yi Geng, Bing Xin, Wu Gui: this is also called **The Ten Stems Strung like Pearls**. Nayin linked through the four positions, generating from above downward or below upward, also qualifies. **Pearls Hanging Downward** includes Dingwei meeting Bingwu hour and Xinchou meeting Gengzi hour. **Direct-Seal Pearl-Linking** includes Renwu meeting Guiwei hour and Jiazi meeting Yichou hour. **Suspended-Seal Pearl-Linking** includes Yihai meeting Jiaxu hour, Guisi meeting Renchen hour, and Dingsi meeting Bingchen hour. Huzhongzi says: “Pearls linked along one line win early renown in all four quarters.” Assistant censor **Peng**, **Dingmao / Wushen / Jihai / Gengwu**, and **Huang Huaqing**, **Jiaxu / Bingzi / Wuwu / Gengshen**, are examples.[^153]

## 102. Earthly Branches Linked like Roots
### 地支連茹
<!-- source: L466-L468 -->
Year, month, day, hour, and conception obtain Zi, Yin, Chen, Wu, Shen, and Xu, each branch separated by one position; or Zi, Chou, Yin, Mao, and Chen in immediate succession. The rhapsody says: “Pulling up grass with linked roots: ever firmer, ever more secure.” Again: “Those obtaining linked roots are rich.” This marks the difference between stems and branches. If both are linked, wealth and rank are complete. The **Duke of Ying**, **Dingsi / Bingwu / Dingwei / Wushen**, and **Liu Yu, Earl of Sincerity**, **Gengzi / Wuyin / Gengchen / Renwu**, are examples. If the year stands two positions away, while month, day, and hour are each one position apart—as a Zi year, Mao month, Si day, and Wei hour—the pattern is called a **Noble-Person Configuration** and is auspicious.[^154]

## 103. The Five Phases’ Direct Seal
### 五行正印
<!-- source: L470-L472 -->
A Jiazi birth obtaining Yichou hour, or a Bingyin birth obtaining Jiaxu hour, exemplifies this. Meeting the native stem is Jia/Yi obtaining Hai–Mao–Wei or Bing/Ding obtaining Yin–Wu–Xu. Carrying the native household is a Yin–Wu–Xu Fire fate obtaining **Bingyin, Wuwu, and Jiaxu**, all native Fire; or Hai–Mao–Wei obtaining **Jihai, Xinmao, and Guiwei**, all native Wood. Finally, Yin–Wu–Xu has Horse at Shen: Shen present and Yin absent; Hai–Mao–Wei has Horse at Si: Si present and Hai absent; Shen–Zi–Chen has Horse at Yin: Yin present and Shen absent; Si–You–Chou has Horse at Hai: Hai present and Si absent. This is **Triple Combination with Horse Replacing the Head Branch**.

## 133. Arching in Salutation toward the Palace Gate
### 拱揖闕門
<!-- source: L590-L592 -->
The Palace Gate is the position opposite the birth year. A Jiazi natal fate takes Jisi and Xinwei as the saluting arch, leaving Wu [午] empty as Palace Gate. Yichou takes Gengwu and Renshen, leaving Wei empty. Infer the others similarly. The various books give special weight to Zi and Wu, less to the others, because Zi and Wu are the Upright Gate and Imperial Seat.[^189]

A yang fate seeing Heavenly Combination, or a yin fate seeing Heavenly Officer and Seal-Ribbon in the two arching day-and-hour positions, qualifies. Jia takes Ji as Heavenly Combination; Xin takes Bing as Heavenly Officer and Ren as Seal. Two Heavenly Combinations, two Heavenly Officers, or two Seals flanking the Palace Gate are also noble; Heavenly Virtue and Tianyi make them more so. Whenever two positions arch an empty position, their stems must be of one kind for a true arch. There are arches of General, Officer, Seat, Nobility, and Seal—not only the Gate. General means the monthly general; Officer means Officer Star; Seat is the hour’s Imperial Seat; Nobility is Tianyi; Seal is the native Seal at Jiaxu, Yichou, Renchen, or Guiwei. Unbroken, they are noble. Lin Kai says: “Sitting upon substance does not equal arching emptiness; open combination does not equal hidden meeting.”

Arching has seventy-two configurations: arching Blade, Harm, Horse, Study Hall, Flying Blade, Robbery Sha, Loss Spirit, ghost, flourishing, breaking, Lonely Star, Widow Lodging, the state council, combination, or ruler-and-minister combination. In each, assess the sha’s weight to distinguish high and low, good and ill, rank and poverty. Jiazi meeting Jiayin, Yichou meeting Yimao, Bingyin meeting Bingchen, Dingmao meeting Dingsi, Wuchen meeting Wuwu, Jisi meeting Jiwei, Gengwu meeting Gengshen, Xinwei meeting Xinyou, Renshen meeting Renxu, or Guiyou meeting Guihai places a useful influence two positions ahead of the natal fate. Useful, it must give distinction; useless, the fate is ordinary. 〈Also called the **Golden Emblem Configuration**.〉[^190]

## 134. Dragon Leaping through Heaven’s Gate
### 龍躍天門
<!-- source: L594-L596 -->
A Xinhai person obtaining Renchen in day or hour, or Renchen obtaining Xinhai, qualifies; Dinghai makes the proper form. Heaven’s Gate lies northwest at Qian. Renchen Water is Direct Seal, and the six dragons ride in governance. Hai encountered therefore brings blessing. Dinghai combines its stem with Ren, whose Lu is at Hai. Those fitting this configuration have achievements that nourish the people and aid the world.

## 135. Tiger Resting in the Dragon Pavilion
### 虎卧龍閣
<!-- source: L598-L600 -->
A Gengshen person obtaining Xinmao in day or hour properly enters the configuration. White Tiger’s home is Gengshen; repeated Xinmao makes the Dragon Pavilion. Hence **Tiger Resting in the Dragon Pavilion**. It indicates distinction, reputation, and an eminent minister whose authority keeps order.

## 136. Clouds Moving and Rain Bestowed
### 雲行雨施
<!-- source: L602-L604 -->
Bingwu or Dingwei obtains Wuzi or Jichou in day or hour; Wuzi or Jichou obtains Bingwu or Dingwei. Bingwu and Dingwei are Heavenly-River Water; Wuzi and Jichou are Thunderbolt Fire. Zi and Wu occupy yin and yang’s proper positions. Their full presence makes yin and yang combine as rain. If the fate already fits a noble configuration, this addition brings nourishing benefit to the people. Even clash, breaking, and Void do not prevent a prefectural or county office.[^191]

## 137. The Clear and Solemn Censorial Terrace
### 清肅憲臺
<!-- source: L606-L608 -->
Si, You, and Chou births all obtaining Yi exemplify this. **Yichou / Yiyou / Yisi / Yiyou** properly enters the configuration. Metal governs punishment; Yi is True Metal, and Si–You–Chou are Metal’s proper positions. Their complete presence above and below often gives censorial or remonstrating office. Traveling Horse in conception or month gives a post in the surveillance commission.[^192]

## 138. Wind and Cloud Meeting in Celebration
### 風雲慶會
<!-- source: L610-L612 -->
For days one through three, obtain Jia or Yin; four through six, Yi or Mao; seven through nine, Bing or Si; ten through twelve, Ding or Wu [午]; thirteen through fifteen, Wu 戊 or Si; sixteen through eighteen, Ji or Wu [午]; nineteen through twenty-one, Geng or Shen; twenty-two through twenty-four, Xin or You; twenty-five through twenty-seven, Ren or Hai; twenty-eight through thirty, Gui or Zi. Assign one stem to each three-day division, then examine the ten stems’ days closely. Jia and Yi meeting Yin and Mao are noble.[^193]

## 139. Doubled Protection and Doubled Officer
### 重䕃重官
<!-- source: L614-L616 -->
Doubled Protection is Jia meeting Gui and then Geng; Doubled Officer is Jia meeting Xin and then Bing. Jia takes Gui as protection, and Gui takes Geng as protection. Jia takes Xin as Officer, and Xin takes Bing as Officer. Jia meeting two Xin is not judged by this method.

## 140. Enclosed by Flags and Banners
### 包裹旗旌
<!-- source: L618-L620 -->
Among the spirits and sha, Robbery Sha is the **Flag**, Loss Spirit the **Banner**. Both must meet to make Flags and Banners; one alone does not suffice. Their enclosure constitutes nobility. **Gengchen / Bingxu / Gengwu / Bingzi** has Robbery Sha at Si and Loss Spirit at Hai. Gengchen and Gengwu arch Robbery Sha in Si, called **Enclosing the Flag**; Bingxu and Bingzi arch Loss Spirit in Hai, **Enclosing the Banner**. Infer similar configurations accordingly. Complete Flags and Banners make even a low-ranking officer a minor supervisor or commander. With Blade, a body controlling sha often beheads others; sha controlling the body must bring injury and death by a blade.[^194]

## 141. How Wealth and Distinction Are Completed
### 富貴所成
<!-- source: L622-L624 -->
A Jia person is born in Hai, Mao, or Wei. Jiachen obtains **Xinhai, Bingyin, and Jihai**; Jiayin obtains **Xinwei, Bingzi, and Jihai**.[^195]

## 142. True Substance Holding Its Position
### 眞體守位
<!-- source: L626-L628 -->
A Ding person obtains Ren within Yin, Mao, Chen, or Hai; or Bing and Xin each occupy flourishing ground, with no Ding elsewhere. These are examples.

## 143. Leaving One Empty for Use
### 虛一待用
<!-- source: L630-L632 -->
The native stems of the four rulers proceed in sequence but skip one character: **Jia–Yi–Bing–Wu**, omitting Ding, for example. Whoever obtains this walks steadily into the azure clouds. But four linked native branches repeating one character—as **Zi–Zi–Chou–Yin**—are heavy at the head and light at the tail, indicating early death.[^196]

## 144. Borrowed Nayin Obtaining the Season
### 假音得時
<!-- source: L634-L636 -->
An Earth person is born in summer’s final month, or occupies Shen–Zi–Chen and travels through the four seasonal-ending regions.

## 145. Treasure, Righteousness, Restraint, and Attack: Four Matters Manifest at Court
### 寶義制伐四事顯朝
<!-- source: L638-L640 -->
The higher generating the lower is **Treasure**; the lower generating the higher, **Righteousness**; the upper controlling the lower, **Restraint**; the lower controlling the upper, **Attack**. These four describe generation and control above and below through conception, month, day, and hour.[^197]

## 146. Five Phases Unmixed, Nine Fates Nourishing One Another
### 五行不雜九命相養
<!-- source: L642-L644 -->
The Three Origins each occupy a region, carry their native Lu, and harmonize; or each occupies flourishing and Storehouse positions, while nayin, stems, and branches generate and nourish one another.[^198]

## 147. The Ruler Flourishing, the Root Complete, Meeting in One Region
### 主旺本成會於一方
<!-- source: L646-L648 -->
Gengzi Earth obtains Bingxu month, Dingchou conception, Gengchen day, and Guiwei hour. Without clash or breaking, they meet in the region of their native qi. Lu and Horse make it particularly fine.[^199]

## 148. Monthly Officer and Virtue Combining, Secretly Meeting Branch Lu
### 月官德合暗逄支禄
<!-- source: L650-L652 -->
Dinghai obtaining Renchen or Renxu, and a Jia person obtaining Chou, Wei, and Hai, are examples.

## 149. Punishment Employed at Its Season, Punishment Guarded without Disorder
### 用刑者有時守刑者不亂
<!-- source: L654-L658 -->
Yin punishing Si in a spring birth makes restraint and control useful. Guisi punishing Wushen without a Ding stem is an example.[^200]

If a person’s natal stems and branches and Four Pillars fit the foregoing configurations, even ruler and root without qi indicate a distinguished name and exceptional standing. The Three Origins obtaining ground give rank; the Four Pillars combining in succession give splendor. With all Three Origins useful and grounded, wealth, rank, and pure distinction are certain. Conception, month, day, and hour exchanging combinations and paying court to the natal fate likewise make a fate of splendid, pure distinction. Only Void, Death, Extinction, and mutual clash are avoided as destruction. If a valid configuration also gathers blessing at the Imperial Seat, appointments of pure distinction must follow. Punishment or breaking progressively reduces the judgment. Heavenly Center with mutual clash and punishment, carrying sha, often gives military office; further Heavenly Center, punishment, and clash indicate repeated suspension and replacement.[^201]

## 150. Examples of Great Nobility Born under the Ten Stems and Twelve Years
### 十干十二年生大貴人例

<!-- source: L660-L662 -->
| Birth-year stem | Month | Day | Hour |
|---|---|---|---|
| Any of the six Jia years | Dingmao | Yiwei | Wuyin |
| Any of the six Yi years | Jimao | Jiaxu | Yihai |
| Any of the six Bing years | Gengyin | Dingsi | Bingwu |
| Any of the six Ding years | Bingwu | Renchen | Dingwei |
| Any of the six Wu years | Renxu | Jichou | Wuyin |
| Any of the six Ji years | Xinwei | Jiwei | Bingyin |
| Any of the six Geng years | Jiashen | Gengshen | Xinsi |
| Any of the six Xin years | Bingshen | Gengwu | Xinsi |
| Any of the six Ren years | Xinhai | Renchen | Dingwei |
| Any of the six Gui years | Bingchen | Bingchen | Wuzi |

For each year, the foregoing gives only one day and one hour at which a greatly noble person is said to come into the world, destined to establish achievements and enterprises; otherwise, an immortal beyond the dust. Ordinary methods supposedly cannot comprehend it.[^202]

Yet no greatly noble person surpasses an emperor. Examining the founding sovereigns of successive dynasties and the emperors of the Ming, I find not one who fits. I have often said: in so vast a world, among so many millions, could there be no one born in these years, months, days, and hours? There must be—but they are not necessarily all greatly noble. When Heaven brings forth a greatly noble person, hidden number and the movement of qi must govern it; year, month, day, and hour are often insufficient grounds for judgment.[^203]

The officials and common people with identical fates whom I have recorded are too many to enumerate. Consider officials alone. Vice Minister **Huang Maoguan** and deputy commissioner **Shen Jie** shared a fate. Huang died in military violence; Shen at home beneath his window. Shen died before Huang; leave aside even the difference in their offices. **Zhu Heng** and **Li Tinglong** shared a fate. Zhu passed the examinations in a Renchen year, Li in a Guichou year. Zhu rose to minister; Li stopped at vice commissioner of provincial administration and did not live long. Leave aside, too, the number and worthiness of their descendants. **Wan Cai** and **Rao Cai** shared a fate. Wan became a metropolitan graduate and rose to senior ministerial office; Rao remained a provincial graduate and became a prefect. Yet Rao had many sons and Wan few; Wan died in penal frontier exile and Rao did not. Their longevity, gains, and losses are difficult to explain as well. **Wang Qiezhai of Sanhe** and his brother were twins, yet the timing of their achievements differed. How many people in the vast world, across the Nine Provinces and among its countless inhabitants, must share the same Eight Characters? How can one judge them all by a single example?[^204]

I remember a tale of a runner who shared **the Duke of Lu’s** fate. When the duke received great imperial favor, the runner received a heavy punishment; when the duke had a small celebration, the runner suffered a slight reprimand. Their responses were thus opposed. Again, a dyer’s family had a son whose fate matched the Duke of Lu’s, sixty years apart. A practitioner compared it with the duke’s chart, to the family’s delight: surely the child would become noble. From childhood they indulged everything he did. He became a drunken wanderer and drowned while intoxicated at only nineteen.[^205]

I also recall an account in the *Record of Delight in Goodness*. Two Imperial Academy scholars had identical fates and passed both the qualifying and departmental examinations together. They agreed to seek official service near one another so each might know the other’s good and ill. One became an instructor at Ezhou, the other at Huangzhou. Before long the Huangzhou man died. The Ezhou man arranged his funeral and prayed: “You and I were born in the same year, month, day, and hour, and our coming forth into service was the same. You have left me first. Even were I to die now, I would already have outlived you by seven days. If you possess awareness, tell me in a dream.” That night he indeed dreamed of the other, who said: “I was born into wealth and rank and enjoyed more than my due; therefore I died. You were born poor and humble and have not yet had such enjoyment; therefore you live.” Later the Ezhou man rose to govern a prefecture. Was this not because he had taken warning and did not enjoy things to excess?[^206]

In my own district, the licentiate **Yan Shoufang** shared a fate with **Yuan Dagang**, a resident of the works settlement. Yan was poor and Yuan rich; Yan had many sons, Yuan only two. Yan is still alive, but Yuan has died. Yan studied, observed ritual propriety, and preserved himself through grave illness, finally entering service through annual tribute selection. Yuan was the reverse. Considering these people together, do not the different households into which they were born and the different pursuits each learned make a difference? Preserving oneself, cultivating conduct with care, practicing frugality, and lengthening one’s years lie in our own seeking of further blessing. To say, “My fate entitles me to wealth, rank, and long life,” while failing to cultivate virtue or advance in learning, acting arrogantly, indulgently, and without restraint—could that be what makes fate truly fate?[^207]

*End of Sanming Tonghui, juan six.*

These are selectively consulted transcriptions, not an independent facsimile collation. The *Gujin* pages transclude underlying page texts, whose later editing is not frozen by the parent-page revision alone; the references identify the consulted parent revisions, not immutable editions of every transcluded character. Agreement among online witnesses does not prove independent descent. S controls inclusion and sequence. English numbering is editorial. The separately transmitted Siku preface remains untranslated.

[^02]: **Several analytical frameworks.** 身, “body,” normally means the day ruler and its strength within the chart, not physical strength alone. The earlier Lu-based and *nayin* frameworks also recur. “Officer” may mean the broad controlling relation or the specifically Direct Officer. “Ghost” denotes a controlling influence functioning harmfully. “Inviting,” “reaching afar,” “clashing forth,” and “arching” describe the text’s inference of an absent branch or stem; they do not mean that the natal chart literally acquires an additional written pillar. 填實, “filling the absent position,” can therefore spoil a configuration built on effective absence. Unambiguous confusion of 己, 巳, and 已 is resolved contextually as Ji, Si, or ordinary wording; consequential uncertainties are noted separately. Contradictory rules are not harmonized into a single contemporary system.

[^03]: **The well-railing’s fork.** S has 井欄斜义; G607 has 井欄斜叉. The graph 义 here represents the fork or crossing, not “righteousness.” The source itself explains the image through a well’s mouth. “Moistening-Downward” translates 潤下, the Water configuration named from Water’s characteristic action. 丙子 and 甲申 are the actual Zi and Shen hours reached on a Geng day; the opening restriction concerns these full hour pillars.

[^04]: **Apparent exceptions are transmitted.** The passage objects to Wu/Ji injuring Water but then expressly permits Wuchen and Wuzi. It welcomes northern Hurting-Officer luck yet criticizes much exposed Ren/Gui. These statements distinguish positions, kinds of presence, and competing configurations without fully specifying every boundary. The translation retains both the restrictions and exceptions rather than supplying a new unified rule. G607 adds two historical examples absent from S; they have not been inserted.

[^05]: **The compiler’s objection.** The quoted rhapsody welcomes Seal, Yin, and filling the position, contrary to the preceding explanation. Wan Minying explicitly challenges it. Both quotation and objection are retained. In particular, the rhapsody’s “filling” has not been changed to “not filling” simply to make its doctrine consistent.

[^06]: **Ji stated, Wu inferred.** S and G607 first identify Ji as Ren’s Officer, then call forth Ding and Wu from Xu. Wu is Ren’s Indirect Officer/Seven Killings, not Ji. Later explanations invoke Jia combining with Ji. “Officer” has therefore been kept broad where the source itself is broad; neither Earth stem has been silently replaced. The term “three wonders” in the subsequent paragraph refers to Wealth, Officer, and Seal, not invariably to the named three-stem series.

[^07]: **Conflicting luck directions.** The first account welcomes bodily strength, while the next explicitly avoids northern Hai/Zi luck. Both readings are shared by G607 and U. No missing qualification has been invented. The later distinction between Dragon-Back, Kuigang, and Moistening-Downward explains why a simple list of universally favorable directions would misrepresent the chapter.

[^08]: **Desire, rank, and wealth.** Shen Cheng’s name serves as an allusion to desire compromising firmness; it is not another technical star. Shi Chong exemplifies fabulous wealth. The Three Terraces and Eight Seats elsewhere similarly stand for exalted civil office, not a literal modern job title. The cited author’s chart judgments are preserved as textual examples; their historical birth data have not been independently authenticated.

[^09]: **Zheng and the Water assembly.** 鄭邦, “the land of Zheng,” is the reading of S and G607. Its precise function in this stanza is not explained; it has not been replaced with an inferred province or branch. The verse’s praise of a complete Water assembly also stands beside the prose’s instruction to classify that assembly separately as Moistening-Downward. The eight-line tune text follows the source’s rhythmic units; 鷓鴣天 is a tune title, not a chapter heading.

[^10]: **A deliberately indirect chain.** The Zi–Si discussion gives three explanations, involving Gui–Wu, Bing–Xin, and Si–You–Chou. They are not abridged into one formula. “Father and son” denotes generation between phase relations. The hidden stems invoked in an imagined palace are kept distinct from stems actually appearing in the Four Pillars.

[^11]: **Gui as wife and Seal.** In the compressed phrase 戊貪合癸妻為印綬, Gui is Wu’s wife through combination and Bing’s Seal through Water generating Fire. The bracketed subject makes the shift of reference explicit without adding a new pillar. The subsequent permission to use visible Officer or Killings is conditional on reclassifying the chart from its monthly command; the text then returns to the stricter remote-Lu configuration.

[^12]: **Conjectural repair in the verse.** S and G607 both read 丑絆子衝, literally “Chou binds, Zi clashes.” The surrounding explanation and next verse identify Wu as the branch clashing with Zi. The translation supplies “[Wu]” on internal evidence; this is not an attested variant of the consulted witnesses.

[^13]: **An unresolved verse reading.** S and G607 have 明怕庚辛二癸侵, including “two Gui.” Two exposed Gui could differ from the Gui concealed in the two Zi, but the verse does not explain this distinction. The reading is retained rather than changed to an expected general term for baleful influences. “Jade Grove” is the imagery of the examination banquet, not a further branch position.

[^14]: **Breaking an absent Si.** 破 here is the special “breaking forth” operation in the configuration, not simply a reference to a modern standardized list of six branch breaks. The text explicitly requires several Chou and an absent Si. Its subsequent instructions reclassify charts with actual Wealth, Officer, Seal, or Arching Lu rather than maintaining remote Si at all costs.

[^15]: **An anomalous Owl in the verse.** 癸嫌戊巳馬蛇梟 ends with 梟, normally Owl/Indirect Seal. The immediately listed Wu, Ji, Horse, and Snake are not all Gui’s Owl. The translation keeps the ominous force without assigning a new uniform Ten-God identity; the transmitted final graph remains an unresolved technical looseness.

[^16]: **Naming the configuration does not settle its use.** The compiler repeatedly allows a monthly Wealth, Officer, or Seal configuration to supersede a special hour-based one. “The six Gui days” means the six valid stem–branch pairs bearing Gui, not six consecutive days. The phrase describing punishment and clash as favorable precedes later restrictions on particular branches; both are retained.

[^17]: **Supported Wu/Xu repair.** S reads 見亥或午戊字為覊絆. G607 reads 午戌: Hai, Wu [午], and Xu can bind Yin through Six or triple combination. Xu replaces S’s Wu 戊 here. The concluding verse’s 舞天 in S is similarly rendered “Flying Heavenly,” supported by G607 飛天 and the immediately preceding comparison.

[^18]: **A damaged summary repaired from G607.** S has 內外無合而有運在平別位 and closes 以上諸格財也. G607 reads 內外無合而有合在乎別位 … 以上諸格是也: combination absent in the actual chart is nevertheless obtained from another position, and the preceding configurations are examples. This supplies a coherent reading without introducing a new luck formula or restricting all these forms to Wealth. 淵源 is retained as the title/tradition *Source*; the phrase “eighteen configurations” is not taken as the number of headings in this juan.

[^19]: **Unresolved Si–You–Chou wording.** S says that You, Xin, and Chou are absent, then has 巳酉合丑. G607 lacks the first 丑 but still transmits the problematic combination clause. The expected account would make Si and Chou combine the invited You, but that transposition is not established by the consulted witnesses. The main text retains S’s sequence, rather than quietly rewriting a rule for practitioners.

[^20]: **Moral analogy, not a diagnosis.** The claim about steadfast Wood borrows ethical language to describe a configuration’s integrity; the marriage analogy likewise expresses the source’s historical hierarchy. Half a jin paired with half a jin signifies equal shares, not a prescription to calculate the stems by physical weight. “Six gates” is transmitted without an explanation here; no six-step method has been supplied.

[^21]: **Two supported Wu repairs, one broad Officer.** S has 戌土 in the Guiyou example; G607 has 戊土, Wu Earth in Chen. In the later Jiayin/Bingwu/Jisi example, S’s 丙戌 is likewise G607 丙戊. Both are rendered with a bracketed Wu. The Guimao example’s Ji in Wu [午] is retained even though Ji is Gui’s Indirect Officer: 官 can be broad here. 破官 is “breaking forth Officer,” not destroying an already operative Officer.

[^22]: **The examples do not all repeat the opening conditions.** The opening requires identical month and day stems, whereas the concluding chart has Ji month and Wu day. Its three Yin nevertheless illustrate the proposed clash. The change from Ren to Geng in the intervening example is explicit in S and G607; it has not been made into one continuous natal chart. Jia and Bing in Yin respectively generate Wealth and supply Wealth for Ren, so “Wealth and the useful influence” preserves the broad wording without calling both Direct Wealth.

[^23]: **Unexpected versus unrighteous wealth.** S has 不意之財, wealth not anticipated; G607 has 不義之財, unrighteous wealth. S’s less moralizing reading controls. The title does not mean ordinary financial loss, despite 破財 having that meaning elsewhere. The listed breaking relationships belong to this configuration’s remote-inference method.

[^24]: **Conjectural title, supported stem repairs.** S, G607, and U all read 虎午奔巳, “Tiger and Wu hastening to Si.” The prose and verse unmistakably concern Yin/Tiger and Chou/Ox; 牛 and 午 are graphically close. “[Ox]” is therefore an explicitly conjectural title repair, not an attested variant. S’s 丙戌 in this and the Goat section is G607/U 丙戊, Bing and Wu in Si; that repair is independently supported.

[^25]: **The prose and verse choose different partners.** The prose asks for You or Chou to retain the invited Si; the verse says You or Shen. Both are retained. This is another reason not to turn a single mnemonic stanza into an exhaustive and internally uniform instruction sheet.

[^26]: **One and three; forked-out opposites.** 得一分三 is retained as “obtaining one and dividing into three.” The examples explain inference through incomplete or complete three-branch assemblies, not arithmetic division of a birth hour. The graph 义 in S again functions as 叉, “fork,” in the ensuing explanation. The author’s warning that such relationships can bring either blessing or calamity is essential.

[^27]: **Honey and poison.** The analogy expresses the hidden conflict of stem combination with branch punishment; it is not medical advice or a claim about an actual substance. G607 expands the analogy with a sexual comparison absent from S; that expansion is not imported. The source’s character judgments are historical assertions, not evidence about individuals.

[^28]: **Which Birth cycle?** Zi as Xin’s Birth ground uses the reverse progression assigned to yin stems, not the undifferentiated forward Metal cycle. Wu 戊 combines with Gui, whereas Wu 午 clashes with Zi; these identically romanized names are disambiguated when both occur. “Officer Star” remains Bing even when absent from the written pillars.

[^29]: **The competing derivations of six yin.** One derivation traces increasing yin from Wu to Hai and returning yang at Zi; another simply counts the six Xin stem–branch days. The text accepts both. The special inclusion of Xinwei and Xinyou is transmitted without a full derivation and has not been generalized to all six days.

[^30]: **Inconsistent directions and alternative careers.** The prose allows northern Hurting Officer after western Metal, while later quotations variously praise the northwest or fear the north. These alternatives are preserved. The passage on grain contributions and handling documents offers routes to status outside the principal examination path; no unsupported exact bureaucratic grade is assigned. S’s 清廟, “pure ancestral court/temple,” differs from G607 清朝, “pure court,” in the True Treasure quotation.

[^31]: **An unresolved contradiction within a stanza.** S and G607 both have 若還子字無相遇, “if no Zi is encountered,” although Wuzi hour defines the form. Chou might be expected, but the witnesses consulted do not establish that correction, so the apparent contradiction remains. The next stanza reads “Chou linked to it” in S; G607 instead says “one more Zi.” These are meaningful variants, not silently interchangeable words.

[^32]: **Rat rather than Monkey.** Zi and Shen are Yi’s Noble-Person branches in the method used here, but the special configuration requires Bingzi hour and an indirect chain through Si and Shen. “Three Wood positions” in the verse refers to the Wood assembly, not three different wood elements. The hour relation should not be substituted for the monthly method when the compiler explicitly chooses the latter.

[^33]: **Flanking can override a stated prohibition.** The two example charts expressly retain Mao or Chou between repeated Zi. Both are translated in full; the exceptions have not been removed to make the initial prohibition universal. Repeated Zi in this form is praised, in contrast to the single Zi required by the preceding Six-Yin form.

[^34]: **Provincial Rose Enclosure.** 藩省薇垣 uses literary language for high provincial administration. It is retained as an institutional image rather than equated mechanically with a modern national prime ministership.

[^35]: **Officer already present.** The line 既帶官星復用此 literally speaks of already carrying Officer Star and still using this form. The following lines condemn the visible Officer/Killings positions, so it can be read as a warning against that mistaken application. The wording is preserved; the translation does not insert “without Officer” to make it a fresh favorable definition.

[^43]: **The third example expands the method.** Yimao/Jiashen/Xinmao/Xinmao extends the opening Jia/Geng explanation to Xin’s absent Lu at You through repeated Mao. The five example charts are retained without being changed to match every introductory branch literally.

[^87]: **Five horses.** 五馬 is an image of high regional office and its carriage, not a requirement for five Traveling-Horse indicators. The chart illustrates Seal generating the body at a further Birth position. Its prohibition concerns control of Seal, not every occurrence of an element that can control something.

[^88]: **Suppression depends on what is suppressed.** A stem above a useful Officer can obstruct it, while Bing above harmful Geng Killings can turn restraint into authority. “Concealed” here includes an influence held down and unable to act, not only an unwritten hidden stem. References to Jiawu, Wuwu, Renwu, and Gengwu concern different relations at the same branch and are all retained.

[^89]: **Competing enumerations of Eightfold Specialization.** The section adds seven names to an earlier list while omitting Dingwei, then singles out four principal Lu days. [^90]: **Examples and biography.** “Master Zhu, Duke Wen” translates 朱文公 as given. [^91]: **Twenty-four directions.** The directional scheme uses eight stems, excluding the two central Earth stems, twelve branches, and four corner trigrams. “Only the first four days” belongs to one explanation; the compiler then returns to the possibility of eight. These successive positions have not been collapsed into a single asserted count.

[^92]: **A different outcome from the same strength.** Here concentrated phase strength is said to favor quiet withdrawal, unlike nearby configurations that promise office. The source’s five-tones wording is retained; it should not be mistaken for a claim that musical tone itself diagnoses sexual behavior. The purity language is part of the historical system.

[^93]: **A phase configuration, not ordinary flexibility of temperament.** 曲直 names Wood’s bending-and-straightening action. The prose requires Hai for Seal, yet permits a separate Metal/Earth interpretation without Hai. The White Emperor in the verse is Metal; Kan is the northern Water domain. These are technical images, not additional unseen pillars.

[^94]: **The Water requirement is genuinely present.** S and G608 both require Hai Water to complete overly flourishing Fire, then warn against Water/Metal country. Both statements are retained. They differ from some later simplified accounts of an exclusively Fire-following configuration; that later doctrine has not been imposed on this text.

[^95]: **Congge is Metal’s characteristic action.** 從革 is rendered Following Transformation to retain its sense of yielding to reform or recasting. It is not identical to the general 從象, Following Image, discussed later. The prose permits a little forging Fire, whereas the verse warns against Fire intermixture; quantity and context remain essential.

[^96]: **The verse adds positions the prose does not systematically require.** Si/Wu and Chen/Xu are expressly named in the first poem, alongside Shen’s authority. Both S and G608 transmit them. [^99]: **Directions depend on the operative refinement.** The passage distinguishes Wood day/Fire refinement, Fire day/Wood refinement, and Fire day/Fire refinement. The three complete charts are retained, including Water in the third chart despite the opening ideal of no injuring Water. Presence alone does not establish injury.

[^100]: **The seal is an image of office.** Casting a seal joins Metal’s material and Fire’s forging action. [^102]: **Natal phase and day ruler are distinguished.** 水命土命 names the natal Water/Earth classification, while 日主 subsequently names the day ruler. The following poems can praise arrangements criticized in the prose; the collection’s disagreement is preserved.

[^113]: **One combined heading, three definitions.** S places three titles together and explains them in one passage; the translation keeps them together. [^114]: **Fraternal and generational images.** 棣萼 evokes brotherly blossoms; the section then extends the pattern to father–son and grandfather–grandson relationships. [^115]: **An excess interpreted as its opposite.** “Many Killings become no Killings” describes a change in how the configuration is used, not deletion of written stems. [^116]: **The source supplies the rescue.** The account links Gui–Wu combination, Xin restraining Yi, and Mao–Xu combination. [^118]: **Different meanings of “turning away.”** Here the term primarily concerns Food/Hurting Officer and the loss of effective Officer, while another cited tradition uses Lu differently. [^120]: **The ten-day interval is textual, not a modern forecast.** The passage refers to the ten days after the summer solstice and the first emergence of yin qi. [^121]: **Winter-solstice yang and transformation.** The Tianshun Emperor’s chart is quoted as the author gives it. Ding–Ren is read as transformed Wood, removing the apparent Water objection in the example. The translation does not independently authenticate the imperial birth record.

[^122]: **A difficult warning is preserved.** S and G608 state that even reaching Birth/flourishing in luck can bring calamity, after first welcoming Wealth and Seal. [^124]: **Intervening positions rather than visible pillars.** Jiazi/Bingyin implies Yichou between them; Renxu/Jiazi implies Guihai. The listed Direct Seal and other indicators include the older nayin and Lu frameworks. Their labels have not been redefined solely by the day stem’s Ten Gods.

[^126]: **Combination need not become transformation.** 合 and 化 are kept distinct. [^129]: **All ten directional accounts are retained.** Jia following Ji is not treated as interchangeable with Ji transforming with Jia; the paired subsections have different preferences. “Concealed within” often refers to a potential combining partner rather than a hidden stem literally stored inside another heavenly stem. The compressed maxims are not expanded into unrecorded natal examples.

[^130]: **Ordinary and transformed relations overlap.** The Geng account calls Ding Officer while discussing transformation into Metal; it is not a newly invented role. Jimao is the complete transmitted pair. The contrast between helpful Ren and destructive Gui is retained as a specific assertion, not generalized into a universal Water rule.

[^131]: **Celestial Balance and Golden Ox.** 天秤 and 金牛 are retained as the source’s astral names. The passage does not supply a sufficiently explicit coordinate scheme to justify converting them into modern zodiac degrees. In the ninth account, 丁臨於乙位, “Ding at Yi’s position,” is shared by S and G609; it has not been changed to a guessed branch.

[^132]: **Returning may mean failed or reversed transformation.** The first explanation describes Metal transformation losing its configuration; the next describes a return to Wood. Both are preserved. Spousal following in the examples is a technical analogy for the relative strength of paired stems, not a prediction of an actual spouse’s conduct.

[^133]: **Two images of light.** Fire beneath obscuring Earth and Fire reflected in Water are distinct explanations under one heading. The four example charts are complete. Gui in the second chart is expressly transformed rather than deleted; no additional missing Water has been supplied to the other charts.

[^134]: **Strength determines the role of restraint.** “Ill Earth,” “declining Fire,” and “dead Wood” describe technical states, not defective physical substances. The text permits a strong body with controlled ghost to become noble while condemning other balances. Its “office through wine” phrase is transmitted without a full mechanism and remains so.

[^135]: **Longevity claims differ within the account.** The maxim allows long life without fulfillment, whereas the conclusion says unaided hidden qi lacks longevity. The examples are preserved rather than altered to reconcile these statements. The “secondary examination list” renders 乙榜 without equating every historical usage with one modern qualification.

[^136]: **Regional affiliation is not literal elemental identity.** Wu/Ji Earth “belonging to Wood” means the day stem’s relation to the dominant branch region. The orange analogy is the source’s illustration of place-dependent qi, not a verified botanical claim. All three charts retain their original stems and branches.

[^137]: **Resemblance can succeed or fail.** 類 can denote a completed gathering of one kind or a merely apparent resemblance to another configuration. The examples intentionally include both failures and noble outcomes. Where Yin–Wu–Xu or Si–You–Chou is named despite a missing member in the written chart, the wording is retained rather than inserting an extra pillar.

[^138]: **Conjectural Shen reading.** S and G609 both have 雖庚甲欲損寅, “although Geng/Jia seek to harm Yin,” although the year pillar is Gengshen and Shen clashes Yin. The translation supplies “[Gengshen]” on that internal evidence, not as an attested variant. The later statement that Jia itself obtains Xin as Officer changes the reference from the woman’s day stem to the husband-star; that shift is retained.

[^139]: **Rootlessness is one account, not the sole opening rule.** The first Following example explicitly gives Gengshen its own flourishing seat. The subsequent rootlessness rule is introduced as another account. The concluding warning about regaining roots concerns disruption of a following configuration, not a universal rejection of supporting qi.

[^140]: **Eight methods, variant definitions.** The source’s first eight image headings and Xuanxu’s eight named methods do not perfectly coincide. Jingshan also reverses the preceding placement of directional and triple assemblies under Kind and Belonging. His definitions are translated in full, not silently aligned with the earlier headings.

[^141]: **The author preserves disagreement.** After quoting Jingshan, the compiler expressly acknowledges different accounts of Concealment and Illumination. That acknowledgment is part of the source, not a translator’s excuse for choosing inconsistent definitions. The reader should not treat these labels as a single fixed taxonomy across every cited authority.

[^142]: **A new source collection begins.** The small note identifies the following material as the *Guide’s* account of proper nobility. Stems combining and branches combining are explained separately before their conjunction. The moral-political imagery of ruler, minister, worthy people, and multitude is integral to the passage.

[^143]: **The song’s list is incomplete.** The mnemonic names Jia, Yi, Bing, Ding, Wu, Xin, Ren, Gui, and Ji, but supplies no separate Geng line. The missing line has not been invented. The four-pillar examples that follow are retained even where they do not transparently fulfill every condition of the opening definition.

[^144]: **Conception and nayin are explicit.** The five-position example includes a separately identified conception pillar, not a fifth natal pillar. The later directional and transformed-qi examples broaden the form beyond five identical nayin. The three historical charts are preserved without adding conception pillars the source does not give.

[^145]: **A low appraisal under a previously noble title.** Earlier Two Stems Unmixed is praised; this short section can give wealth’s “copper smell” and low office, with little examination fame. The different appraisal is transmitted. It has not been reconciled by an invented distinction between alternating and paired order.

[^146]: **Supported Wu repair.** S’s 三壬一戌 gives “three Ren and one Xu,” mixing a branch into a stem sequence. G609 reads 三壬一戊, “three Ren and one Wu.” The bracketed Wu follows that witness. The exceptions for Earth and Metal, and the three nayin examples, are all retained.

[^147]: **The source disputes its own conception method.** The opening counts conception among the five nayin positions; Huzhongzi explicitly rejects adding conception merely to complete the Five Phases. Both arguments and the two charts are included. No calculation is offered that pretends to make these methods identical.

[^148]: **Sixth position supplied in more than one way.** Lu/Horse or the Life Palace may supplement the usual four pillars and conception. The title therefore does not name six fixed natal pillars. The author’s own example expressly supplies Lu and Horse at Shen.

[^149]: **Quotation boundary uncertain.** The passage introduces the *Huainanzi* and continues into a developed account of essence, qi, and spirit. It does not clearly delimit how much is direct quotation. The translation quotes the opening attribution and presents the subsequent exposition separately, without claiming the whole paragraph has been verified against a critical *Huainanzi* edition.

[^150]: **Four non-Earth stems on each side.** The yang list omits Wu and the yin list Ji, as transmitted. The four Tomb Sha and six ominous generals are then different classificatory systems. The number “six” is not filled with two invented names when the ensuing explanation names only four generals.

[^151]: **Four names under “six.”** Soaring Snake, Vermilion Bird, White Tiger, and Dark Warrior are the only generals named in the explanation. Their defeats are described through elemental restraint. These images should not be equated automatically with the five directional day configurations earlier in the juan.

[^152]: **Advancing in qi can move backward in sequence.** Bingyin–Dingmao and its reversal illustrate this explicitly. The stem/branch order is therefore preserved rather than rearranged into one forward sequence. The Noble-Person pairing uses the source’s stated distribution of yin and yang noble branches.

[^153]: **Several kinds of linkage share the title.** The first two five-stem sequences are complementary, but the following combination list omits Ding–Ren; it is not completed editorially. The two example charts also illustrate different kinds of ordered succession rather than one identical sequence. The nayin and Seal variants are retained separately from ordinary stem order.

[^154]: **A supported year reading.** S has the defective 子午卯月 in its last example; G609 reads 子年卯月, “Zi year, Mao month,” followed by Si day and Wei hour. The translation follows that division. The earlier list of six alternate branches describes a sequence, not six natal pillars. The grass-root image emphasizes connection and stability.

[^155]: **Direct Seal in the older nayin framework.** Jiazi/Metal meeting Yichou/Metal in its Storehouse and Bingyin/Fire meeting Jiaxu/Fire are not simply ordinary Day-Master resource relations. The opening excludes Void, while the closing allows it in another formulation. Both qualifications are preserved.

[^156]: **Eight pairs, not four birth charts.** The concatenated source examples are divided into eight encounters. The heading’s “yang Death, yin Birth” does not describe the first four in the same direction: the explanation itself gives Geng/Jia Birth with Xin/Yi Death. The final compressed condition requires reciprocal participation rather than isolated same-phase Birth; its wording has not been expanded into a new calculation.

[^157]: **Supported title repair, unresolved numbers.** S’s 一旬也裹 is G609 一旬包裹, “enclosed within one round.” The sequence of four, five, or seven positions, twenty-four unities, thirty-six positions, and the Great Expansion’s empty one is shared by both witnesses but insufficiently explained. The numbers are preserved without fabricating a usable arithmetic procedure from them.

[^158]: **A supported transposition.** S prints 巳己 in the four-pair example; G609 has 己巳, Jisi. This restores a valid sexagenary pair. The source defines five positions but supplies only four examples here; no conception pillar has been invented. The alternative of each position occupying a different round is expressly a separate configuration.

[^159]: **Earth branches in the imperial example.** The opening foregrounds Wu and Ji, but the Ming Taizu chart includes Ren and Ding as well. Its explanation turns on the four Earth branches and the completeness of Noble-Person positions. The chart is retained as the source gives it; the imperial birth data and the astrological explanation have not been independently authenticated.

[^160]: **Self-Birth, self-Flourishing, and self-Tomb use nayin.** These are properties of complete stem–branch pairs, not merely of their exposed stems. Conception is separately identified where supplied. In the Four-Seal example, both Bingchen and Renchen remain Chen; the Jiaxu conception completes the set. No natal branch has been changed to make a tidier four-pillar arrangement.

[^161]: **Two different accounts of the same fate.** The long quotation gives Dingmao luck with a Bingyin year; the later explanation gives Bingchen luck with a Dingmao year and death at fifty-six. Both are preserved rather than merged into a reconstructed chronology. S’s defective 生時主永主 is clarified by G609 生時主末主: the hour governs the final part of life. The claim that Bingyin’s stem, branch, and nayin are all Fire is transmitted even as the next clause recognizes the Wood beneath it; the sentence has not been silently converted into a conventional hidden-stem table.

[^162]: **The second chart is a comparison, not a continuation.** The Wujiang assistant magistrate’s chart illustrates two stem transformations and two branch combinations; the subsequent alternative explanation returns to Han’s Ji day. The two people and charts are kept distinct. The name 袁韶 has not been identified with the unrelated, homophonous historical name 袁紹.

[^163]: **Grouping the transmitted sequences.** The punctuation separates the forward and reverse strings into their opening five-stem sequence and subsequent three-stem groups. No stems are added or omitted. The terse distinction between “forward numerical order” and “mixed numbers” remains a source limitation; it does not establish a new numerical weighting formula.

[^164]: **Dinghai Earth is nayin.** Dinghai’s Earth classification differs from Ding’s ordinary Fire. The account of the six Chen positions is transmitted without a full derivation. “Blending harmony” renders 沖和 here; it is not the destructive branch clash denoted by 衝 elsewhere.

[^165]: **Before and behind are relative to the natal year.** The opening’s “three positions” names a range but illustrates two positions within it. Later examples vary the distance. The translation preserves those examples rather than imposing one consistent inclusive-counting convention. “Leading” and “Following” describe their relationship to the natal position, not an instruction to reorder the birth pillars.

[^166]: **A derived Gate configuration.** The Renchen/Renyin pair arches Mao; the text then invokes Dingyou and the Proper Upright Gate. It does not supply a fifth natal pillar or a complete new chart. The compact reference is retained, and the author expressly says this is not ordinary Leading and Following.

[^167]: **The source leaves its own example unsettled.** Yang Bo’s chart is first explained favorably through two Geng Officers and their positions, but the final small-character note says his Leading and Following is not fully explained. That reservation is retained. It matters because the two branches do not transparently exemplify the preceding preference for distant Leading and near Following.

[^168]: **Enclosing the round is not twelve natal pillars.** The two examples place Xu, Hai, Zi, and Chou at points of enclosure. The statement about twelve positions refers to the extent of the enclosing sequence, not twelve independently recorded birth positions. The source gives no further calculation, and none is supplied.

[^169]: **The example illustrates one generating four.** Xinhai is Metal nayin; the other four positions, including the explicitly given conception pillar, are Water nayin. The opening maxim permits both four-generating-one and one-generating-four, while this chart demonstrates the latter. The title does not restrict the passage to the former.

[^170]: **The Jiashen is inferred.** It is the hidden combining partner, not a fifth natal pillar added to Xie’s chart. The stem-tracing to Jimao and the shared Ji of Lu and Horse supply the source’s explanation; it has not been recast as a modern Day-Master Wealth/Officer configuration.

[^171]: **Four groups of day stems and hour branches.** The mnemonic gives three possible day stems for each of Yin, Si, Shen, and Hai hours. It does not list four complete natal charts. The two actual examples are separately retained, without supplying any unrecorded positions.

[^172]: **The example is explicitly a hidden configuration.** The short poem names Horse and Rooster encounters, while Lin’s example is explained through the absent Xu, Zi, and Yin flanked within the chart. The translation retains that explanation instead of changing the hour to make a literal reading of the poem fit.

[^173]: **Tracing is not merely ordinary phase generation.** The three successive results Guihai, Jiayin, and Xinwei are given with a complete example. 遁 and 推 describe the source’s tracing procedure; the paragraph does not provide enough additional detail to reconstruct a separate general algorithm, and the translation does not invent one.

[^174]: **Different levels of the image.** Yin/Tiger and Chen/Dragon first function as branch images; Wuchen and Jiayin then invoke complete pairs and their nayin relationships. The example and the general verse need not use precisely the same level. “Wind follows Tiger; cloud follows Dragon” is literary-cosmological imagery, not a prediction of actual weather.

[^175]: **Country and lodging remain distinct.** The ten paired-branch cases flank a stem’s own Lu position. The subsequent grouped-stem cases are called Lu’s Lodging and rank above them in this account. The text then distinguishes a Storehouse encountered at birth from a Tomb encountered in annual progress. These differences are retained rather than reduced to one formula.

[^176]: **Wealth’s phase and its Storehouse.** For Water nayin, Fire is Wealth; for Wood nayin, Earth is Wealth. The text’s compressed 財庫 wording connects that Wealth with its storage position; it does not mean that every Fire or Earth branch is itself a Storehouse. Renchen Water injuring Fire Wealth and Wuchen Wood injuring Earth Wealth explain the subsequent “ghost within” warning.

[^177]: **Grouped stems, not one ten-character succession.** Jia–Wu–Geng, Yi–Ji, Bing–Ding, Ren–Gui, and Xin are Noble-Person stem groups in the system used here. The reference to four successive stems concerns their disposition through the pillars. It has not been rendered as a requirement to fit all ten stems visibly into four positions.

[^178]: **More than one kind of flanking.** Some examples place branches on either side of an absent Lu or Noble Person; others place the native body between its Lu and Noble-Person positions. The text expressly says the latter need not carry the extra branch spirits visibly. These are different explanatory arrangements, not contradictory transcriptions of one chart.

[^179]: **A favorable name is not a complete configuration.** The author explicitly says that four noble positions alone do not make the Noble-Person configuration. The surrounding requirements of phase coherence, qi, and supporting influences are therefore retained, rather than treating the star names as sufficient evidence of rank.

[^180]: **A surname’s five-tone classification.** The attribution of Chen to zhi/Fire belongs to the historical five-tone surname system; it is not a modern phonetic etymology or an independently verified causal explanation. The example also permits high office without examination fame, distinguishing those two kinds of success.

[^181]: **Conjectural final negation.** S and G609 repeat 乘生旺之氣 in the final contrast, first saying one favorable feature suffices and then that two are needed. “[Without]” supplies a probable missing negation from that contrast, not an attested variant. The preceding hierarchy explicitly values the stem/ruler/root above nayin while still allowing damaged nayin to impair the result.

[^182]: **Mixed ordinary and nayin phase language.** S correctly gives Metal and Water for Gengshen/Jiashen in its stated grouping; G609 instead prints Metal and Wood. S’s reading controls. Other pairs employ ordinary stem relations alongside nayin, so the whole sequence should not be reduced to a table using only one level of phase identity.

[^183]: **Unity is a pattern of convergence.** “The One” is the organizing principle toward which otherwise different influences return. The many examples that follow place the decisive single influence in different positions; this is not a rule that exactly one named star must occur in every such fate.

[^184]: **The source’s alternatives remain alternatives.** The phrases “one Metal with four Water-and-Earth” and “one Water with four Metal-and-Earth” are compressed and shared by G609. They are preserved as mixed groupings rather than silently rewritten as five identical nayin. Conception, year, day, and hour can each supply the exception in the different examples.

[^185]: **Sun and moon, month and day.** 日月 operates within both the cosmological analogy and the arrangement of pillars. The English distinguishes the sun and moon moving through emptiness from the month and day occupying the chart’s middle. The configuration accommodates an adverse middle position rather than pretending it is absent.

[^186]: **Ten complete-pair positions are preserved.** The five self-Flourishing and five self-Birth examples concern nayin, not simply the visible stem’s ordinary phase. The instruction to judge from the hour is expressly conditional on the other positions lacking qi; it has not been generalized to all charts.

[^187]: **Reciprocal recovery in nayin.** Xinyou is Wood nayin on Metal’s branch; Guimao is Metal nayin on Wood’s branch. Their exchange restores each phase to its own flourishing position. “The preceding reign” preserves the compiler’s historical vantage when mentioning Muzong; it is not a statement about any present officeholder. The imperial and ministerial charts are the source’s examples, not newly authenticated birth records.

[^188]: **Five listed forms, then a further variant.** The tripod image expresses the stability of threefold assembly. The paragraph enumerates five applications and adds Horse Replacing the Head Branch; all are translated. The warning against Metal in the Fire assembly makes clear that the presence of threefold structure alone is not sufficient.

[^189]: **The Gate is opposite the birth year.** The examples deliberately leave Wu or Wei unoccupied between the arching positions. In the subsequent compressed distinction, Jia/Ji illustrates Heavenly Combination, while Xin/Bing illustrates Heavenly Officer and Xin/Ren Seal. The translation follows those explicit examples rather than treating all three relations as interchangeable.

[^190]: **A claimed total, not a complete enumeration.** The source says there are seventy-two arching configurations but names only a selection. No missing configurations have been manufactured to reach that total. The concluding ten Golden-Emblem encounters are all preserved and distinguished from the preceding selective list.

[^191]: **Nayin supplies the clouds-and-thunder image.** Heavenly-River Water and Thunderbolt Fire are the named nayin of the complete pairs. The image then supports a claim about beneficent government; it is not an instruction to infer rainfall from a natal chart.

[^192]: **Yi as True Metal.** This uses the transformed or “true” phase associated with Yi–Geng, not the ordinary classification of Yi as Wood. The all-Yi example is therefore preserved without substituting a Metal stem. The reference to punitive authority belongs to the source’s account of Metal and censorial office.

[^193]: **Ten three-day divisions.** All ten groups, covering days one through thirty, are retained. The paragraph does not explicitly settle the calendrical basis or give a modern date-conversion rule. These ordinal days have not been converted to Gregorian dates or zodiac degrees.

[^194]: **Technical Flags and Banners.** The titles name Robbery Sha and Loss Spirit. The two pairs of pillars arch their absent positions at Si and Hai; they do not indicate literal flags observed at birth. The violent outcomes are the text’s historical predictions, not evidence of a living person’s conduct.

[^195]: **The final miniature entries are complete as transmitted.** Several headings are followed by only one sentence or a string of examples. Their brevity is not an abridgment in the translation. Missing premises and unstated calculations are not filled by conjectural exposition.

[^196]: **Omission and repetition are different cases.** The favorable example skips one stem in a sequence; the unfavorable example repeats a branch at the beginning. The opposed judgments therefore apply to different structures. Neither example is expanded into a complete invented birth chart.

[^197]: **Positional hierarchy.** Higher/lower and upper/lower identify the relationship of the compared positions, not the moral worth of people. Treasure, Righteousness, Restraint, and Attack are the four technical names supplied by the text. Their application to conception, month, day, and hour is retained without adding a fifth relation.

[^198]: **Nine appears only in the title.** The source does not enumerate nine fates here. It gives a brief condition concerning the Three Origins, native Lu, and mutual generation. No nine-item system has been reconstructed from this fragment.

[^199]: **A native region need not mean five identical nayin.** The complete example includes different nayin and all four Earth branches. The conditional “without clash or breaking” remains the source’s qualification despite the branches’ potential oppositions; no branch is altered to remove that tension.

[^200]: **Useful punishment is conditional.** The first example specifies spring as the season in which Yin’s punishment of Si is useful. The second adds the absence of Ding to Guisi/Wushen. The source gives no fuller derivation, so the translation does not supply an unstated chart or promote the condition into a universal favorable-punishment rule.

[^201]: **The Imperial Seat and the military exception.** The hour is again the focal Imperial Seat where blessings can gather. Heavenly Center denotes technical Void; 右職 is rendered military office in this context. The author permits a valid larger configuration to qualify the unfavorable judgment of a ruler/root lacking qi, rather than treating weakness alone as decisive.

[^202]: **Ten rows, with transmitted inconsistencies.** The heading says “Ten Stems and Twelve Years,” but the body organizes ten stem groups, each referring to its six sexagenary years. It does not explain the twelve. All ten month–day–hour combinations are preserved. The Wu-year row gives Jichou day and Wuyin hour, although the ordinary hour-stem rule used elsewhere would give Bingyin on a Ji day. S and G609 share Wuyin; it has not been silently repaired. The compiler’s ensuing criticism also prevents treating this table as an endorsed prediction schedule.

[^203]: **The compiler tests and questions the rule.** The claim that no founding sovereign or Ming emperor matches the table is his reported examination, not an independent survey carried out for this translation. He rejects the sufficiency of identical Eight Characters while retaining belief in hidden number and qi. The conclusion is neither an unqualified defense of chart determinism nor a wholesale rejection of the art.

[^204]: **Names, ranks, and sexagenary years.** Huang Maoguan is a personal name ending in 官, not “Huang Mao held office.” The name is corroborated by the account of the murdered grain-administration vice minister in the [imperially annotated historical chronicle, juan 109](https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/御批歴代通鑑輯覽_(四庫全書本)/卷109). This does not independently authenticate every matched birth chart in the paragraph. Li Tinglong’s 大參 is rendered vice commissioner of provincial administration; the offices are not flattened into one modern rank. The sexagenary examination years remain unconverted because the passage does not supply a unique full calendar date for each.

[^205]: **Two reported cautionary tales.** The Duke of Lu is not personally identified in this passage, and no conjectural name is supplied. The runner’s inverse outcomes and the dyer’s indulged son are the author’s reported stories, not independently verified demonstrations. The sixty-year separation is essential: the second case concerns a repeating sexagenary pattern, not simultaneous birth.

[^206]: **A dream within the cited narrative.** The *Record of Delight in Goodness* story is translated with its reported dream intact. Neither the dream’s authenticity nor its explanation of unequal longevity has been established here. Its role is to support the compiler’s warning against excessive enjoyment and mechanical prediction from identical birth pillars.

[^207]: **The closing appeal to cultivated conduct.** 厰民 is kept broadly as a resident of the works settlement; the passage does not establish a modern factory occupation. Annual tribute selection is an educational route into service, not the metropolitan examination degree. The final appeal to restraint, learning, and self-cultivation belongs to the author’s moral account of fate. It is not evidence that poverty, disability, illness, or early death demonstrate a person’s moral failure.
