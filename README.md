# 三命通會 — Siku Quanshu edition

A complete twelve-juan Markdown export of the Chinese Wikisource transcription specifically labelled **三命通會 (四庫全書本)**. The repository name `Sanming-Tongshui` is retained as requested; the work is conventionally romanized *Sanming tonghui*.

The `source/` directory contains **exactly twelve UTF-8 Markdown files**, one per juan. The Siku editorial notice **提要**, published separately on Wikisource's work page, is included at the beginning of `source/juan-01.md`, so it is not omitted and no thirteenth text file is needed.

## English translation

[Read juan one in English](translation/juan-01.md). The complete juan is translated, **excluding the separate Siku editorial preface**, which remains untranslated. The English body is approximately **41,800 words**, followed by **134 translator’s endnotes**. It includes the philosophical debates, all sixty concise entries and their fuller judgments, all thirty detailed nayin essays, and the concluding Five-Phase and seasonal discussions.

[Read juan two in English](translation/juan-02.md). The complete juan is translated in approximately **41,200 words**, followed by **162 translator’s endnotes**. Its 31 editorially numbered sections cover stems and branches, calendrical and astronomical models, the conception origin and life palace, major and minor luck cycles, the annual influence, combinations, transformations, harms, punishments, and clashes. The separate Siku preface remains untranslated.

[Read juan three in English](translation/juan-03.md). The complete juan is translated in approximately **38,100 words**, followed by **147 translator’s endnotes**. Its 28 editorially numbered sections cover Lu and Horse, Noble Persons, literary and protective indicators, the principal spirits and sha, and the full concluding verse-and-commentary discussions of their interactions. All **136 concluding verse stanzas** are retained. A substantial displacement between the Lu and Traveling Horse passages is restored with comparison-text support and documented in the notes; genuine gaps and inconsistent enumerations remain identified rather than filled by invention.

[Read juan four in English](translation/juan-04.md). The complete juan is translated in approximately **40,500 English words**, followed by **116 translator’s endnotes**. Its 10 editorially numbered sections include the ten-stem analyses, all twelve monthly-branch discussions, seasonal and regional judgments, and the full verse-and-commentary sequences. The five paired-stem discussions preserve **120 four-line stanzas**; the concluding true-number and nayin material preserves another **62**, all with their transmitted commentary. Ordinary stem phases, transformed or “true” phases, and nayin are distinguished rather than merged into a single system. The separate Siku preface remains untranslated.

[Read juan five in English](translation/juan-05.md). The complete juan is translated in approximately **41,000 English words**, followed by **223 translator’s endnotes**. Its 13 editorially numbered sections cover the origins of the technical names, Direct and Indirect Officer, Wealth, Seal, Inverted Food, Mixed Qi, Hurting Officer, Food God and its related configurations, Yang Blade, and Established Lu. The named configurations, example charts, prose qualifications, and verse collections are retained. Supported repairs are distinguished from conjectures and unresolved readings; incomplete enumerations and conflicting authorities are not silently harmonized. The separate Siku preface remains untranslated.

[Read juan six in English](translation/juan-06.md). The complete juan is translated in approximately **38,800 English words**, followed by **207 translator’s endnotes**. Its **150 editorially numbered sections** preserve the named configurations, alternative interpretations, example charts, verse collections, and five reference tables. The complete closing discussion of people with identical Eight Characters but different lives is retained, including the compiler’s cautions about rigid prediction and his appeal to cultivated conduct. The separate Siku preface remains untranslated.

[Read juan seven in English](translation/juan-07.md). The complete juan is translated in approximately **36,700 English words**, followed by **265 translator’s endnotes**. Its **9 editorially numbered sections** cover the history and meaning of Ziping, character and appearance, illness, poverty and misfortune, longevity, women’s fates, children, the Six Relations, and pregnancy divination. All prose, example charts, verse collections, and accompanying commentary are retained, including the full kinship discussions and the concluding trigram and arithmetic methods. Conflicting authorities, incomplete examples, and disputed readings remain visible. The separate Siku preface remains untranslated.

[Read juan eight in English](translation/juan-08.md). The complete juan is translated in approximately **42,300 English words**, followed by **172 translator’s endnotes**. Its **60 day–hour sections** cover Jia, Yi, Bing, Ding, and Wu days through the twelve hours, preserving **360 individual day judgments** and **2,163 year/month catalogue captions** with their accompanying comments. These captions are not a count of distinct people: repeated charts and shared-chart comparisons remain. The main text preserves **239 verse groups, totaling 1,192 lines**, including the short summaries printed as commentary. An eight-line song absent from the Siku transcription is translated in an endnote rather than inserted into the main text. The separate Siku preface remains untranslated.

[Read juan nine in English](translation/juan-09.md). The complete juan is translated in approximately **41,800 English words**, followed by **177 translator’s endnotes**. Its **60 day–hour sections** cover Ji, Geng, Xin, Ren, and Gui days through the twelve hours, preserving **360 individual day judgments** and **2,112 year/month catalogue captions** with their accompanying comments. These captions are not a count of distinct people; shared charts, alternative accounts, and repeated records remain. The main text preserves **240 verse groups totaling 1,200 lines**, including the short summaries printed as commentary. The closing note on the original and added records, duplicates, and errors of hearsay is translated in full. The separate Siku preface remains untranslated.

[Read juan ten in English](translation/juan-10.md). The complete juan is translated in approximately **36,100 English words**, followed by **153 translator’s endnotes**. Its **three treatises** are *Formulas for Reading a Fate*, *Wuxian’s Collected Essentials*, and *Profound Instructions of the Jade Well*. All **167 Jade Well maxim-and-commentary units**, the quoted verse passages, example charts, and the compiler’s concluding afterword are retained. The endnotes preserve disagreements between authorities, distinguish ordinary and transformed phase methods, and document supported corrections and unresolved readings. A quatrain present only in the parallel witness is translated in an endnote, not inserted into the Siku text. The separate Siku editorial preface remains untranslated.

The repository’s Siku text remains the controlling source. Doubtful readings were selectively compared with the parallel material in the *Gujin Tushu Jicheng*, Arts juan 593–622, and the shorter unlabelled Wikisource versions. For juan two, selected passages were also checked directly against images of the 1909 *Gailiang Sanming Tonghui*, juan 2; the endnotes identify the witnesses and relevant scan pages. For juan three, related quotations in the *Wuxing jingji* and a primary lexical parallel also inform specific repairs. Juan four also compares relevant *Wuxing jingji* passages; its endnotes give revision-pinned witnesses and distinguish supported repairs from conjecture. Juan five selectively compares Arts juan 604–606 and the unlabelled Wikisource version; its first endnote also explains the revision limits of transcluded comparison texts. Juan six compares Arts juan 607–609 and the unlabelled version, documenting supported variants, explicit conjectures, and unresolved readings; its first endnote likewise distinguishes parent-page revisions from the separately editable texts transcluded into them. Juan seven selectively compares Arts juan 601, 611, and 612 and the unlabelled version; a facsimile page supports the repair of Xu Juyi’s name, and the notes identify other supported variants and unresolved readings. Juan eight selectively compares Arts juan 613–615 and the unlabelled version; its notes distinguish repaired headings and catalogue readings, conjectures, unresolved fragments, and material present only in a parallel witness. Juan nine selectively compares Arts juan 616–619 and the unlabelled version. Its notes distinguish witness-supported repairs from conjectures and internal emendations, retain incomplete case records, and explain the source’s competing day–hour assignments and its own acknowledgement of added material. Juan ten selectively compares Arts juan 620–622, with a direct image check of selected final passages in volume 472, leaf 26 recto. Particular historical and literary readings are also checked against the primary sources identified in its endnotes. The source’s own objection to an interpretation, its distinctive Void rule, and the limits of uncertain chronology remain visible. These are parallel textual witnesses, not additional Siku editions. The endnotes distinguish attested variants, conjectural repairs, and unresolved readings; they also explain terminology and changes of analytical framework. The original Chinese source files are unchanged. This is not a complete line-by-line collation against historical facsimiles.

## What is preserved

The files in `source/` contain the source text, not summaries or translations. All text inside each source page's `onlyinclude` / `poem` region is retained, including the inline notes, source section labels, closing volume labels and classification lines. The order of the source lines is preserved; leading indentation and excess blank lines are normalized. Standalone source anchors become Markdown headings. Wikisource's `SK notes` content is displayed with its text-export delimiters, `〈…〉`.

Rare-character placeholders are expanded using Wikisource's own `SKchar` mapping at export time. **57 distinct mappings** resolve to Unicode. **26 distinct image-only glyphs**, occurring **126 times**, are retained as inline SVG images with identifying alternative text. Their files are stored in `assets/glyphs/`, outside `source/`; they are glyph images, not font files. Keep the assets directory with the Markdown for offline rendering. The filenames, source URLs, checksums and Commons attribution records are in `SOURCE_MANIFEST.json`.

Wikisource navigation, site-interface text, HTML styling and copyright banners are not part of the book text and are excluded. Per-file YAML metadata and HTML region markers are added for provenance and mechanical validation.

## Verification and scope

All **13 textual source bodies** (the preface and twelve juan) were checked in three forms: revision-pinned wikitext, the saved Markdown, and MediaWiki-rendered HTML for the same revision. After removing whitespace and added Markdown heading markers, and treating each matching image ID as a glyph token, their character sequences match exactly. The saved source files also have SHA-256 checksums recorded in `SOURCE_MANIFEST.json`.

This establishes a complete, loss-checked export of the named **Wikisource transcription**. It is **not** a fresh transcription or an independent line-by-line collation against historical page scans. Existing transcription errors, uncertain readings and source conventions are retained rather than silently corrected. The edition label follows Wikisource's source identification.
